35+ Gingerbread Desserts Everyone Will Love

Oct 26, 2026

When the house smells like ginger, cinnamon and molasses, you know the holidays have truly arrived: gingerbread desserts are here. From candy-covered gingerbread houses and spiced layer cakes to bundt cakes, yule logs, cupcakes and every cookie shape you can cut, these gingerbread desserts cover every corner of the season.

Work through this list of 35 gingerbread desserts one weekend at a time, or pick your favorites for a dessert table that guests will remember long after the holidays. Every dessert below comes with simple ingredients and easy directions, so even a first-time baker can fill the house with something wonderful.

1. Classic Gingerbread Men

Soft, spiced gingerbread men with smiling faces are the cookie that starts every holiday baking season. They are sturdy enough to decorate and tender enough to eat by the handful.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 tablespoon ground ginger
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup molasses
  • 1 egg
  • Royal icing, to decorate

Directions

  1. Whisk the flour, baking soda, ginger and cinnamon, then beat in the butter, sugar, molasses and egg.
  2. Chill the dough 1 hour, roll out, cut into men and bake at 350°F for 10 minutes.
  3. Cool completely and decorate with royal icing.

4. Classic Gingerbread House

A candy-covered gingerbread house is the centerpiece every Christmas table deserves. Build it slowly, decorate it wildly, and let everyone admire it before the first bite.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups flour
  • 1 tablespoon ground ginger
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 3/4 cup molasses
  • 1 egg
  • Royal icing, for glue and decoration
  • Assorted candies

Directions

  1. Make a firm gingerbread dough, cut out house walls and roof pieces and bake at 350°F for 12 minutes.
  2. Cool the pieces completely, then glue the house together with thick royal icing and let it set.
  3. Decorate the roof and walls with icing and candies.

5. Gingerbread House Cake

A rustic spiced cake crowned with a tiny gingerbread house brings the whole village to the dessert table. It is a showstopper that tastes as good as it looks.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 tablespoon ground ginger
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 3/4 cup molasses
  • 2 eggs
  • Cream cheese frosting
  • 1 mini gingerbread house, to top

Directions

  1. Beat the butter, sugar, molasses and eggs, fold in the dry ingredients and bake in a round pan at 350°F for 35 minutes.
  2. Cool completely and frost with cream cheese frosting.
  3. Set the mini gingerbread house on top with pine sprigs.

16. Gingerbread Yule Log

A chocolate yule log rolled around spiced cream and topped with snowflake gingerbread cookies is the holiday cake everyone remembers. It looks like a winter forest floor.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 4 eggs, separated
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 cup whipped cream
  • Chocolate ganache
  • Snowflake gingerbread cookies, to top

Directions

  1. Whisk the yolks and sugar, fold in the dry ingredients and stiff egg whites, then bake in a lined jelly-roll pan at 350°F for 12 minutes.
  2. Roll warm in a towel, unroll, spread with whipped cream and roll again.
  3. Cover with ganache, texture with a fork like bark and top with snowflake cookies.

Gingerbread Desserts Tips

  • Chill gingerbread dough for at least 1 hour before rolling — cold dough holds its shape and gives crisp edges.
  • For houses, bake the pieces a day ahead so they dry firm, and use thick royal icing as glue so the walls set solid.
  • Roll cookie dough a little thick for soft centers, or thin for snappy cookies that are perfect for decorating.
  • Store gingerbread in an airtight tin and it stays fresh for a week — the spice flavor even gets better after a day or two.
