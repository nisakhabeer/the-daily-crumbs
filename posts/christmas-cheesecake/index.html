35+ Christmas Cheesecake Everyone Will Love

Oct 26, 2026

When the house smells like vanilla and the tree lights are on, only one dessert feels truly right: Christmas cheesecake. From cranberry-topped classics and peppermint candy cane slices to eggnog, gingerbread and chocolate versions, these Christmas cheesecake recipes cover every flavor the season brings.

Work through this list of 35 cheesecakes one weekend at a time, or pick your favorites for a dessert table that guests will remember long after the holidays. Every cheesecake below comes with simple ingredients and easy directions, so even a first-time baker can fill the house with something wonderful.

1. Classic New York Cheesecake

Tall, dense and silky, the classic New York cheesecake is the one every holiday table needs. A buttery graham crust and a hint of lemon make it timeless.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 32 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1 teaspoon lemon zest

Directions

  1. Press the crumbs and butter into a springform pan and chill 20 minutes.
  2. Beat the cream cheese, sugar, eggs, vanilla and lemon zest until smooth and pour over the crust.
  3. Bake at 325°F for 55 minutes, cool, then chill 4 hours before slicing.

2. Strawberry Santa Hat Cheesecake

Tiny cheesecakes topped with strawberry Santa hats are almost too cute to eat. They are the first thing children grab from the dessert table.

Ingredients

  • 12 mini graham cracker crusts
  • 16 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 12 strawberries, hulled
  • Whipped cream, to pipe
  • Powdered sugar, for dusting

Directions

  1. Beat the cream cheese, sugar, eggs and vanilla until smooth and divide among the mini crusts.
  2. Bake at 325°F for 18 minutes, cool and chill 2 hours.
  3. Pipe a whipped cream brim, set a strawberry on top, add a cream pom-pom and dust with powdered sugar.

3. Cranberry Christmas Cheesecake

A ruby cranberry topping over creamy cheesecake tastes like Christmas in every bite. The tart berries balance the rich filling perfectly.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 32 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 cups fresh cranberries
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup orange juice

Directions

  1. Make and bake the cheesecake at 325°F for 55 minutes, then cool and chill.
  2. Simmer the cranberries, sugar and orange juice until the berries pop and the sauce thickens.
  3. Cool the sauce, spoon over the chilled cheesecake and chill 1 hour before serving.

4. Chocolate Truffle Cheesecake

Dark chocolate ganache over a chocolate cheesecake base makes this taste like a giant truffle. It is the richest slice on the whole list.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups chocolate cookie crumbs
  • 1/4 cup melted butter
  • 24 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 8 oz dark chocolate, melted
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla

Directions

  1. Press the cookie crumbs and butter into a pan and chill.
  2. Beat the cream cheese, sugar, eggs, melted chocolate and vanilla until smooth and bake at 325°F for 50 minutes.
  3. Heat the cream, pour over extra chopped chocolate, stir into ganache and spread over the cooled cheesecake.

5. Peppermint Candy Cane Cheesecake

Crushed candy canes folded into a creamy cheesecake give every slice a cool mint crunch. It tastes like the holidays and looks like a snowstorm.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups graham cracker crumbs

Directions

14. Basque Burnt Cheesecake

Baked hot until deeply caramelized on top, Basque cheesecake is creamy in the middle and dramatic on the table. No crust, no fuss, all flavor.

Ingredients

  • 32 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • Berry sauce, to serve

Directions

  1. Beat the cream cheese, sugar, eggs, cream, flour and vanilla until completely smooth.
  2. Pour into a parchment-lined pan and bake at 425°F for 35 minutes until deeply browned.
  3. Cool, chill 4 hours and serve with berry sauce.

Christmas Cheesecake Tips

  • Bake cheesecake in a water bath or with a pan of water in the oven to stop cracks and keep the center creamy.
  • Cool the cheesecake slowly in the turned-off oven, then chill at least 4 hours — overnight is even better.
  • Slice with a long knife dipped in hot water and wiped clean between cuts for perfect, smooth slices.
  • Make cheesecakes ahead and freeze them whole, then add fresh toppings on the day for the freshest finish.
