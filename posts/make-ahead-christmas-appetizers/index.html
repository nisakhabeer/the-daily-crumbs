25+ Make Ahead Christmas Appetizers for Stress-Free Hosting

Oct 24, 2026

The secret to a calm Christmas party is appetizers that are already done before guests arrive. These make ahead Christmas appetizers chill, freeze or hold beautifully, so on party day you only plate, bake or garnish — no last-minute cooking chaos.

Work through this list of make ahead Christmas appetizers a few days early, label everything, and keep a simple party-day timeline. A little planning now means you can actually enjoy your own party, glass in hand, while the oven does the last bit of work.

1. Cranberry Brie Bites

Buttery puff pastry cups hold molten brie and ruby cranberry sauce, finished with a piney whisper of rosemary. They look festive, taste rich, and disappear fast.

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
  • 8 oz brie, cut into small cubes
  • 1/2 cup cranberry sauce
  • 1 tablespoon fresh rosemary, chopped
  • 1 egg, beaten, for brushing

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Cut the pastry into 24 squares and press into a mini muffin pan.
  2. Place a cube of brie in each cup, top with a teaspoon of cranberry sauce and a pinch of rosemary, and brush the edges with egg.
  3. Bake 12 to 15 minutes until puffed and golden.
  4. Make-ahead: Assemble and freeze up to 1 month in a sealed container; bake from frozen at 400°F (200°C) for 16 to 18 minutes, with no thawing needed.

2. Spinach Artichoke Dip

Warm, creamy and scoopable, this classic dip is the quiet hero of the appetizer table. Serve it bubbling with crostini, crackers and crisp vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 10 oz frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
  • 1 can (14 oz) artichoke hearts, drained and chopped
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 3/4 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • 1 clove garlic, minced

Directions

  1. Stir the spinach, artichokes, cream cheese, sour cream, parmesan, half the mozzarella and garlic together.
  2. Spoon into a baking dish and top with the remaining mozzarella.
  3. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 20 to 25 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  4. Make-ahead: Make 3 days ahead and chill, covered, in the refrigerator; bake before serving, adding 5 minutes to the baking time if chilled.

3. Cheese Ball

A retro favorite that still earns its place, rolled in toasted nuts and fresh herbs for crunch and color. These make ahead Christmas appetizers are at their best when the flavors have a day or two to settle.

Ingredients

  • 16 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded sharp cheddar
  • 1/2 cup chopped pecans or walnuts, toasted
  • 2 tablespoons fresh herbs, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Crackers, for serving

Directions

  1. Beat the cream cheese, cheddar, garlic powder and half the herbs until smooth.
  2. Shape into a ball, wrap tightly, and chill until firm.
  3. Roll in the nuts and remaining herbs just before serving.
  4. Make-ahead: Shape 5 days ahead and chill, wrapped, in the refrigerator; roll in nuts on the day of serving and let stand 20 minutes before serving.

4. Stuffed Mushrooms

Earthy mushroom caps filled with a savory, breadcrumb-topped mixture are a two-bite wonder. They bake quickly and suit almost every guest at the table.

Ingredients

  • 24 large mushrooms, stems removed and chopped
  • 1/2 lb sausage or 10 oz spinach, cooked and drained
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

Directions

  1. Cook the chopped stems with garlic in olive oil, then stir in the sausage or spinach, breadcrumbs and parmesan.
  2. Fill each mushroom cap generously and place on a lined baking sheet.
  3. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 18 to 20 minutes until golden.
  4. Make-ahead: Stuff 2 days ahead and chill, covered, in the refrigerator; bake on the day of serving, adding 3 to 5 minutes if chilled.

5. Mini Quiches

Golden, custardy and endlessly adaptable, mini quiches feel special without any fuss. Make a mixed batch — bacon and cheese for some, spinach for others — and let guests choose.

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet pastry or 24 mini tart shells
  • 3 eggs
  • 3/4 cup cream or milk
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1/2 cup cooked bacon or spinach, chopped
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Press pastry into a mini muffin pan and divide the bacon or spinach and cheese among the cups.
  2. Whisk the eggs, cream, salt and pepper, and pour over the fillings.
  3. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 16 to 18 minutes until set and golden.
  4. Make-ahead: Bake, cool and freeze up to 1 month in a sealed container; reheat from frozen at 350°F (175°C) for 12 to 15 minutes.

6. Deviled Eggs

Cool, creamy and classic, deviled eggs are the bite guests always reach for first. A dusting of paprika and a fresh herb finish keep them looking party-ready.

Ingredients

  • 12 eggs, hard-boiled and peeled
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon paprika, plus more to garnish
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Chives, to garnish

Directions

  1. Halve the eggs, scoop out the yolks, and mash with mayonnaise, mustard, paprika, salt and pepper until smooth.
  2. Pipe or spoon the filling back into the whites.
  3. Chill until serving, then dust with paprika and scatter with chives.
  4. Make-ahead: Fill 1 day ahead and chill, covered, in the refrigerator; garnish on the day of serving so the tops stay fresh.

7. Bacon-Wrapped Dates

Sweet dates, crunchy almonds and crisp bacon make a salty-sweet bite that needs no sauce. They are simple, sturdy and made for passing around the room.

Ingredients

  • 24 Medjool dates, pitted
  • 24 whole almonds
  • 12 slices bacon, halved crosswise
  • 24 toothpicks

Directions

  1. Tuck an almond into each date and wrap with a half slice of bacon, securing with a toothpick.
  2. Place seam-side down on a lined baking sheet.
  3. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 15 to 18 minutes, turning once, until the bacon is crisp.
  4. Make-ahead: Wrap 2 days ahead and chill, covered, in the refrigerator; bake on the day of serving straight from the fridge.

8. Caprese Skewers

Fresh mozzarella, sweet tomatoes and basil, brightened with balsamic, bring a cool contrast to richer holiday food. They also add welcome red, white and green to the table.

Ingredients

  • 24 small mozzarella balls
  • 24 cherry tomatoes
  • 24 fresh basil leaves
  • 2 tablespoons balsamic glaze
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Thread a tomato, a basil leaf and a mozzarella ball onto each short skewer.
  2. Arrange on a platter and drizzle with olive oil and balsamic glaze.
  3. Season lightly and serve chilled or at cool room temperature.
  4. Make-ahead: Assemble on the morning of serving and chill up to 8 hours, covered, in the refrigerator; drizzle with balsamic just before serving.

9. Shrimp Cocktail

Chilled shrimp with a zesty cocktail sauce feels elegant and effortless. It is light, refreshing and always the first platter to empty.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs large shrimp, peeled and deveined, tails on
  • 1 cup cocktail sauce
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  • 1 tablespoon horseradish, optional
  • Fresh parsley, to garnish

Directions

  1. Simmer the shrimp in salted water for 2 to 3 minutes until pink, then plunge into ice water and drain.
  2. Stir the horseradish into the cocktail sauce, if using.
  3. Arrange the shrimp around the sauce with lemon wedges and parsley.
  4. Make-ahead: Cook 2 days ahead and chill, covered, in the refrigerator; plate on the day of serving and keep chilled until guests arrive.

10. Meatballs in Cranberry Sauce

Saucy, glossy and sweet-tart, these meatballs simmer in a cranberry glaze that tastes unmistakably like Christmas. Set them in a slow cooker on warm and let guests help themselves.

Ingredients

  • 24 cooked meatballs, beef or pork
  • 1 can (14 oz) cranberry sauce
  • 1/2 cup chili sauce
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon mustard

Directions

  1. Stir the cranberry sauce, chili sauce, brown sugar and mustard together in a pan.
  2. Add the meatballs and simmer 15 to 20 minutes until hot and glazed.
  3. Serve warm with toothpicks.
  4. Make-ahead: Cook and freeze up to 1 month in sauce; reheat gently in the sauce until piping hot.

11. Crostini with Toppings

Crisp toasts are a blank canvas for bruschetta, mushrooms or cheese, so one batch can become three different bites. Keep toppings separate and let the table look abundant.

Ingredients

  • 1 baguette, sliced 1/2-inch thick
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 cup bruschetta topping
  • 1 cup sautéed mushrooms
  • 1 cup soft cheese or whipped feta
  • Fresh herbs, to garnish

Directions

  1. Brush the baguette slices with olive oil and toast at 400°F (200°C) for 6 to 8 minutes until golden.
  2. Cool completely and store in an airtight container.
  3. Top with bruschetta, mushrooms or cheese just before serving.
  4. Make-ahead: Toast 2 days ahead and store airtight at room temperature; top on the day of serving so the toasts stay crisp.

12. Pinwheels

Colorful spirals of cream cheese, deli meat and spinach slice neatly and travel well. They are the easiest way to fill a platter with very little last-minute work.

Ingredients

  • 4 large tortillas
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 8 slices deli meat
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 teaspoon mustard, optional

Directions

  1. Spread each tortilla with cream cheese and mustard, then layer with deli meat, spinach and cheese.
  2. Roll tightly, wrap, and chill until firm.
  3. Slice into 1-inch pinwheels and arrange on a platter.
  4. Make-ahead: Roll 2 days ahead and chill, wrapped, in the refrigerator; slice on the day of serving for clean edges.

13. Jalapeño Poppers

Creamy, cheesy and just spicy enough, these poppers bake into crisp, golden halves. A bacon crumble on top adds crunch and a smoky finish.

Ingredients

  • 12 jalapeños, halved and seeded
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar
  • 4 slices bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • 1/4 cup breadcrumbs

Directions

  1. Stir the cream cheese, cheddar and half the bacon together.
  2. Fill each jalapeño half and sprinkle with breadcrumbs and the remaining bacon.
  3. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 15 to 18 minutes until golden and bubbling.
  4. Make-ahead: Fill 2 days ahead and chill, covered, in the refrigerator; bake on the day of serving straight from the fridge.

14. Sausage Rolls

Flaky pastry around seasoned sausage is pure party comfort. Cut them small, bake them golden, and watch them vanish before the main event.

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 lb sausage meat
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 teaspoon mustard
  • 1 egg, beaten, for brushing
  • Sesame seeds, optional

Directions

  1. Mix the sausage meat with thyme and mustard, then shape into logs along the pastry.
  2. Roll, seal, brush with egg, sprinkle with sesame seeds and cut into pieces.
  3. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 20 to 22 minutes until deep golden.
  4. Make-ahead: Assemble and freeze up to 1 month, unbaked; bake from frozen at 400°F (200°C) for 25 to 28 minutes.

15. Bruschetta Topping

Juicy tomatoes, basil and garlic spooned over crisp bread is a bright, fresh start to any party. Make the topping ahead and the bread stays perfectly crisp.

Ingredients

  • 4 large tomatoes, diced and drained
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
  • 1 baguette, sliced and toasted

Directions

  1. Stir the tomatoes, basil, garlic, olive oil and balsamic together and season to taste.
  2. Chill until ready to use, then drain any excess liquid.
  3. Spoon over freshly toasted baguette slices just before serving.
  4. Make-ahead: Make the topping 2 days ahead and chill, covered, in the refrigerator; toast the bread on the day of serving.

16. Smoked Salmon Blinis

Delicate blinis topped with smoked salmon, cream cheese and dill feel luxurious with very little effort. They are ideal for a drinks-first kind of evening.

Ingredients

  • 24 small blinis
  • 8 oz smoked salmon
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 tablespoon fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  • Black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Spread each blini with cream cheese.
  2. Top with a folded slice of smoked salmon and a sprinkle of dill.
  3. Finish with black pepper and serve with lemon wedges.
  4. Make-ahead: Make the blinis 2 days ahead and chill, covered, in the refrigerator; assemble on the day of serving.

17. Stuffed Peppers Bites

Sweet mini peppers filled with creamy, herby cheese bake into colorful, poppable bites. They hold their shape well and add a festive splash of color.

Ingredients

  • 24 mini sweet peppers, halved and seeded
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 2 tablespoons fresh herbs, chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder

Directions

  1. Stir the cream cheese, shredded cheese, herbs and garlic powder together.
  2. Fill each pepper half and place on a lined baking sheet.
  3. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 14 to 16 minutes until soft and lightly golden.
  4. Make-ahead: Fill 2 days ahead and chill, covered, in the refrigerator; bake on the day of serving straight from the fridge.

18. Cocktail Sausages

Sticky, sweet and savory cocktail sausages in a barbecue glaze are a guaranteed crowd-pleaser. Keep them warm in a small slow cooker for easy grazing.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs cocktail sausages
  • 1 cup barbecue sauce
  • 1/4 cup grape jelly or brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon mustard

Directions

  1. Stir the barbecue sauce, jelly or sugar and mustard together in a pan.
  2. Add the sausages and simmer 15 minutes until glazed and hot.
  3. Serve warm with toothpicks.
  4. Make-ahead: Cook, cool and chill 3 days in the refrigerator; reheat gently in the glaze until hot.

19. Hummus & Veggie Cups

Individual cups of hummus with crisp vegetable sticks are neat, portable and refreshingly light. They are a thoughtful option for guests who want something fresh.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups hummus
  • 12 small cups
  • 2 carrots, cut into sticks
  • 2 celery stalks, cut into sticks
  • 1 cucumber, cut into sticks
  • Paprika, to garnish

Directions

  1. Spoon hummus into the bottom of each cup.
  2. Stand a few vegetable sticks upright in each cup.
  3. Dust lightly with paprika and chill until serving.
  4. Make-ahead: Make the hummus 5 days ahead and chill, covered, in the refrigerator; assemble the cups on the morning of serving.

20. Cranberry Meatballs

These glossy meatballs in a cranberry glaze are a second, freezer-friendly take on the holiday classic — a little tangier, a little saucier, and just as easy to serve. These make ahead Christmas appetizers are perfect for a slow-cooker table.

Ingredients

  • 24 meatballs
  • 1 can (14 oz) whole-berry cranberry sauce
  • 1/2 cup cranberry juice or chili sauce
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon orange zest

Directions

  1. Stir the cranberry sauce, juice or chili sauce, honey and orange zest together.
  2. Add the meatballs and simmer 15 to 20 minutes until glazed.
  3. Serve warm with toothpicks.
  4. Make-ahead: Freeze up to 1 month in the glaze; reheat gently until hot, stirring to loosen the glaze.

21. Cheese Straws

Flaky, cheesy twists with a gentle paprika warmth are dangerously snackable with a drink in hand. Bake a big tin and let guests graze all evening.

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 cup finely shredded sharp cheddar
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • Sesame seeds, optional

Directions

  1. Brush the pastry with egg and scatter with cheddar, paprika and sesame seeds.
  2. Cut into 1/2-inch strips and twist each strip several times.
  3. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 12 to 15 minutes until crisp and deep golden.
  4. Make-ahead: Bake 1 week ahead, cool completely, and store in an airtight tin at room temperature; recrisp 3 minutes in a hot oven if needed.

22. Mini Tacos

Small, crisp tacos with seasoned meat and fresh toppings bring a fun, build-your-own feel to the table. Keep the filling warm and the toppings cool for the best contrast.

Ingredients

  • 12 mini tortillas
  • 1 lb seasoned ground meat or shredded chicken
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • Fresh cilantro, to garnish

Directions

  1. Cook the seasoned meat until hot and well browned.
  2. Warm the tortillas and fill with meat, cheese and tomatoes.
  3. Top with sour cream and cilantro.
  4. Make-ahead: Make the filling 3 days ahead and chill, covered, in the refrigerator; reheat and assemble on the day of serving.

23. Antipasto Skewers

Salami, cheese, olives and artichokes on a pick pack a whole antipasto board into one bite. They chill beautifully and need no cooking at all.

Ingredients

  • 24 slices salami, folded
  • 24 cubes cheese
  • 24 olives
  • 1 cup marinated artichoke hearts, halved
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano

Directions

  1. Thread salami, cheese, an olive and an artichoke piece onto each skewer.
  2. Arrange on a platter and drizzle with olive oil.
  3. Sprinkle with oregano and chill until serving.
  4. Make-ahead: Assemble 1 day ahead and chill, covered, in the refrigerator; bring to cool room temperature 20 minutes before serving.

24. Baked Brie

A whole brie wheel wrapped in puff pastry with jam and nuts bakes into a molten, show-stopping centerpiece. Cut it open at the table for the best reaction.

Ingredients

  • 1 brie wheel (about 8 oz)
  • 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
  • 1/4 cup jam, such as cranberry or apricot
  • 1/4 cup chopped nuts
  • 1 egg, beaten, for brushing
  • Crackers, for serving

Directions

  1. Spread jam over the brie, sprinkle with nuts, and wrap in pastry, sealing the edges.
  2. Brush with egg and place seam-side down on a lined sheet.
  3. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 20 to 25 minutes until deep golden.
  4. Make-ahead: Wrap 2 days ahead and chill, covered, in the refrigerator; bake on the day of serving, adding 5 minutes if chilled.

25. Festive Cheese Tree

Cheeses, meats and fruit arranged as a tree turn the appetizer table into a centerpiece. It is festive, generous and surprisingly simple to build.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cheese cubes, mixed varieties
  • 1 cup cured meats, folded
  • 1 cup grapes and berries
  • 1 foam cone, wrapped in plastic wrap
  • Fresh rosemary sprigs
  • Toothpicks
  • Crackers, for serving

Directions

  1. Set the wrapped cone upright on a platter and secure it.
  2. Starting at the bottom, pin cheese cubes, folded meats and fruit to the cone with toothpicks, working in rows to form a tree shape.
  3. Tuck rosemary sprigs into gaps and top with a small star of cheese or a berry cluster.
  4. Surround with crackers and serve.
  5. Make-ahead: Prep the components 2 days ahead and chill, covered, in the refrigerator; assemble the tree on the morning of serving and chill until guests arrive.

Make Ahead Tips

  • Label everything with the date it was made and the finish step — future-you will be grateful.
  • Freeze flat on trays before bagging so bites don’t stick together.
  • Underbake freezer items slightly and finish baking day-of for fresh taste.
  • Keep a written party-day timeline: what bakes, what plates, what garnishes, in order.

Final Thoughts

With these make ahead Christmas appetizers ready in the fridge or freezer, hosting stops feeling like a race and starts feeling like a celebration. Choose a mix of hot, cold, creamy and crisp bites, follow your timeline, and enjoy the party as much as your guests do.
