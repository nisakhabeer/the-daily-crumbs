35+ Christmas Brunch Recipes That Steal the Morning Show

Oct 26, 2026

Christmas morning deserves a table that feels as festive as the tree beside it, and these Christmas brunch recipes are exactly how you get there. From classic eggs benedict and spiced shakshuka to fluffy pancakes, berry French toast, buttery pastries and sparkling berry mimosas, this list covers every craving your holiday table will hold.

Work through these 35 Christmas brunch recipes one weekend at a time, or pick your favorites for a slow Christmas morning everyone remembers. Every recipe below comes with simple ingredients and easy directions, so even a first-time brunch host can fill the house with something wonderful.

1. Classic Eggs Benedict

Buttery toasted muffins, salty bacon, perfectly poached eggs and silky hollandaise make this the brunch classic everyone orders. It looks fancy but comes together in one calm rhythm.

Ingredients

  • 4 English muffins
  • 8 slices bacon
  • 4 eggs
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Pinch of cayenne
  • Chives, to garnish

Directions

  1. Cook the bacon until crisp and toast the muffins.
  2. Poach the eggs for 3 minutes in gently simmering water.
  3. Whisk the yolks, lemon juice and melted butter into hollandaise, then stack muffins, bacon, eggs and sauce.

2. Spiced Shakshuka

Eggs poached right in a smoky tomato and pepper sauce make shakshuka the one-pan brunch that begs for warm bread. It is bold, saucy and made for sharing straight from the skillet.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 red pepper, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 4 eggs
  • Feta and bread, to serve

Directions

  1. Soften the onion, pepper and garlic in olive oil.
  2. Stir in the tomatoes and spices and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Crack in the eggs, cover and cook until just set, then serve with bread.

3. Chicken and Waffles

Crispy fried chicken on a golden waffle with a pour of maple syrup is the sweet-salty brunch that never fails. Make the waffles fresh so they stay crisp under the chicken.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken thighs
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 2 cups waffle batter
  • Butter
  • Maple syrup

Directions

  1. Soak the chicken in buttermilk, coat in seasoned flour and fry until golden.
  2. Cook the waffles until crisp.
  3. Stack the chicken on the waffles with butter and maple syrup.

4. Flaky Butter Croissants

Layers of golden, shattering pastry and soft buttery centers make croissants the quiet star of any brunch table. Warm them before serving and watch them disappear.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups flour
  • 1 tablespoon yeast
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 1/2 cups cold butter
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • Pinch of salt

Directions

  1. Make a yeasted dough and fold in the cold butter in three turns.
  2. Roll, cut into triangles and roll into crescents.
  3. Proof for 1 hour, brush with egg and bake at 400°F for 18 minutes.

5. Smoked Salmon Bagel Sandwich

Cream cheese, silky smoked salmon, capers and dill on a toasted bagel is brunch elegance with almost no cooking. It is the fastest way to make a holiday morning feel special.

Ingredients

  • 4 bagels
  • 8 oz cream cheese
  • 8 oz smoked salmon
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons capers
  • Fresh dill
  • Lemon wedges

Directions

  1. Toast the bagels and spread generously with cream cheese.
  2. Layer smoked salmon, onion and capers.
  3. Top with dill and a squeeze of lemon.

6. Open-Faced Egg and Cheese Sandwich

Thick toast, melted cheese and a fried egg make this open-faced sandwich the cozy, knife-and-fork brunch everyone loves. A little hot sauce on top never hurts.

Ingredients

  • 4 thick bread slices
  • 4 slices cheese
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Salt and pepper
  • Hot sauce, optional

Directions

  1. Toast the bread and melt cheese over each slice.
  2. Fry the eggs in butter until the whites set.
  3. Slide an egg onto each slice and season well.

7. Berry French Toast

Custardy French toast piled with strawberries and blackberries is Christmas morning on a single plate. A dusting of powdered sugar makes it look straight from a café.

Ingredients

  • 8 thick bread slices
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • Butter, for frying
  • Mixed berries
  • Powdered sugar

Directions

  1. Whisk the eggs, milk, vanilla and cinnamon.
  2. Dip each slice and fry in butter until golden.
  3. Top with berries and a dusting of powdered sugar.

8. Avocado Breakfast Skillet

Sizzling avocado, spinach and eggs in one skillet make a fresh, green brunch that still feels hearty. Serve it straight from the pan with crusty bread.

Ingredients

  • 2 avocados, sliced
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Chili flakes
  • Bread, to serve

Directions

  1. Warm the olive oil and garlic, then wilt the spinach.
  2. Add the avocado slices and crack in the eggs.
  3. Cover and cook until the eggs set, then finish with chili flakes.

9. Avocado Toast with Poached Eggs and Smoked Salmon

Creamy smashed avocado, a jammy poached egg and ribbons of smoked salmon turn simple toast into a café-worthy brunch. A squeeze of lemon ties it all together.

Ingredients

  • 4 bread slices
  • 2 ripe avocados
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 oz smoked salmon
  • Lemon juice
  • Chili flakes
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Toast the bread and smash the avocado with lemon and salt.
  2. Poach the eggs for 3 minutes.
  3. Spread avocado on toast and top with salmon and an egg.

10. Classic Avocado Toast

Thick toast, ripe avocado and a pinch of flaky salt prove that the simplest brunch is often the best. Add a drizzle of olive oil and chili flakes for the café finish.

Ingredients

  • 4 bread slices
  • 2 ripe avocados
  • 1 lemon
  • Olive oil
  • Chili flakes
  • Flaky salt

Directions

  1. Toast the bread until deeply golden.
  2. Smash the avocado with lemon juice and salt.
  3. Pile onto toast and finish with olive oil and chili flakes.

11. Poached Eggs on Toast

A runny poached egg over buttered toast is the timeless brunch that never needs dressing up. Keep the water gentle and the yolks will stay gloriously golden.

Ingredients

  • 4 bread slices
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon vinegar
  • Butter
  • Salt and pepper
  • Chives

Directions

  1. Toast and butter the bread.
  2. Simmer water with vinegar and poach each egg for 3 minutes.
  3. Drain, place on toast and season with salt, pepper and chives.

12. Berry Yogurt Parfait with Granola

Layers of creamy yogurt, fresh berries and crunchy granola make a light, jewel-toned start to a heavy holiday brunch. Build them in glasses so everyone sees the layers.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups yogurt
  • 2 cups mixed berries
  • 1 cup granola
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • Mint leaves

Directions

  1. Spoon yogurt into glasses.
  2. Layer with berries and granola.
  3. Drizzle with honey and top with mint.

13. Full English Breakfast

Sausages, bacon, beans, eggs and toast on one big plate make the full English the heartiest brunch of the list. It is a feast best served with strong tea.

Ingredients

  • 4 sausages
  • 4 bacon rashers
  • 1 can baked beans
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tomatoes, halved
  • 4 toast slices
  • Butter

Directions

  1. Fry the sausages and bacon until golden.
  2. Warm the beans and grill the tomatoes.
  3. Fry the eggs and pile everything onto warm plates with toast.

14. Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes

Tall, golden and impossibly fluffy, buttermilk pancakes are the stack every Christmas brunch needs. Keep them warm in the oven while you finish the batch.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • Butter and syrup

Directions

  1. Whisk the dry ingredients, then stir in the buttermilk and eggs.
  2. Rest the batter for 5 minutes.
  3. Cook on a buttered griddle until bubbly, flip and serve with syrup.

15. Strawberry Pancakes

Fresh strawberries folded right into the batter make these pancakes blush pink and taste like summer on a winter morning. Top with more berries and a pour of syrup.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup sliced strawberries
  • Butter
  • Syrup

Directions

  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, milk and eggs into a batter.
  2. Fold in the strawberries.
  3. Cook on a buttered griddle and serve with extra berries and syrup.

16. Savory Spinach and Cheese Pancakes

These savory pancakes swap syrup for melted cheese, spinach and cherry tomatoes, making them the brunch for guests who skip sweets. They are hearty, colorful and surprisingly light.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup spinach
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Seeds, to top

Directions

  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, milk and eggs.
  2. Stir in the spinach and cheese.
  3. Cook on a griddle and top with tomatoes and seeds.

17. Fruit and Granola Breakfast Bowl

A chilled bowl of oats, granola and fresh fruit is the bright, crunchy counterpoint to a rich brunch spread. Let guests build their own with extra toppings.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups oats
  • 1 cup granola
  • 2 cups mixed fruit
  • 1 cup yogurt or milk
  • Honey, to drizzle

Directions

  1. Divide the oats and yogurt between bowls.
  2. Top with fruit and granola.
  3. Drizzle with honey and serve chilled.

18. Assorted Breakfast Pastries

A platter of golden pastries — croissants, danishes and swirls — turns brunch into a bakery visit without leaving home. Buy the best bakery box you can find and warm them gently.

Ingredients

  • Assorted pastries (croissants, danishes, cinnamon swirls)
  • Butter
  • Jam
  • Powdered sugar

Directions

  1. Warm the pastries in a low oven for 5 minutes.
  2. Arrange on a large platter.
  3. Serve with butter, jam and a dusting of sugar.

19. Breakfast Sandwiches with Hash Browns

Egg, cheese and sausage tucked into a toasted bun with a crisp hash brown on the side is the handheld brunch guests grab first. Wrap them in parchment for a diner feel.

Ingredients

  • 4 buns
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 sausage patties
  • 4 cheese slices
  • 4 hash browns
  • Butter

Directions

  1. Cook the sausage patties and fry the eggs.
  2. Toast the buns and melt cheese over the patties.
  3. Build the sandwiches and serve with crisp hash browns.

20. Continental Breakfast Spread

Fresh bread, butter, jam and hot coffee make the continental spread the simplest, most elegant way to feed a crowd. Add fruit and cheese and it becomes a feast.

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf fresh bread
  • Butter
  • Assorted jams
  • Cheese
  • Fresh fruit
  • Coffee

Directions

  1. Slice the bread and arrange on a board.
  2. Add butter, jams, cheese and fruit.
  3. Serve with plenty of hot coffee.

21. Mediterranean Breakfast Board

Olives, dips, cheeses, eggs and warm bread on one board bring sunny Mediterranean flavors to a cold Christmas morning. Guests love grazing their way through it.

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs, boiled
  • 1 cup olives
  • 1 cup hummus
  • Cheese wedges
  • Cucumber
  • Tomatoes
  • Warm flatbread

Directions

  1. Boil the eggs and slice.
  2. Arrange the olives, dips, cheese and vegetables on a board.
  3. Add warm flatbread and serve.

22. Sunny-Side-Up Eggs with Bacon and Toast

Glistening sunny-side-up eggs, crisp bacon and buttered toast are the diner classic that anchors any brunch table. Keep the yolks runny for dipping.

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • 4 toast slices
  • Butter
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Fry the bacon until crisp and drain.
  2. Fry the eggs sunny-side-up in the same pan.
  3. Serve with buttered toast and season well.

23. Loaded Breakfast Platter

A big platter piled with croissants, cheese, eggs and breakfast sides lets everyone build their perfect plate. It is the easiest way to feed a crowd without a seating plan.

Ingredients

  • 4 croissants
  • 4 eggs, scrambled
  • Cheese slices
  • Fruit
  • Butter
  • Jam

Directions

  1. Scramble the eggs softly.
  2. Warm the croissants.
  3. Pile everything onto a large platter with butter and jam.

24. Chocolate-Glazed Donuts with Cream

Soft donuts under a glossy chocolate glaze with a dollop of cream are the sweet treat that makes brunch feel like a celebration. They disappear faster than anything else on the table.

Ingredients

  • 6 plain donuts
  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • 2 tablespoons cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Whipped cream, to serve

Directions

  1. Melt the chocolate with cream and butter into a glaze.
  2. Dip each donut and let set.
  3. Serve with a dollop of whipped cream.

25. Berry Mimosas

Pink, bubbly and crowned with fresh raspberries, berry mimosas are the toast every Christmas brunch deserves. Keep the juice chilled so the fizz stays lively.

Ingredients

  • 1 bottle sparkling wine, chilled
  • 2 cups berry juice, chilled
  • Fresh raspberries
  • Mint

Directions

  1. Chill the glasses in the freezer.
  2. Pour half juice and half sparkling wine.
  3. Drop in raspberries and a mint leaf.

26. Cheesy Scrambled Eggs

Soft, creamy scrambled eggs folded with melted cheese are the comfort dish that grounds the whole brunch table. Low heat and patience make them silky.

Ingredients

  • 8 eggs
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1/4 cup cream
  • Salt and pepper
  • Chives

Directions

  1. Whisk the eggs with cream and salt.
  2. Cook gently in butter, stirring slowly.
  3. Fold in the cheese off the heat and top with chives.

27. Festive Brunch Platter

Waffles, pancakes, bacon and a bottle of bubbly on one showstopper platter turn brunch into a party. Set it in the center and let guests help themselves.

Ingredients

  • 4 waffles
  • 4 pancakes
  • 8 bacon rashers
  • Berries
  • Maple syrup
  • Sparkling wine

Directions

  1. Cook the waffles and pancakes and keep warm.
  2. Fry the bacon until crisp.
  3. Arrange on a large platter with berries, syrup and bubbly.

28. Scrambled Eggs and Croissants

Buttery croissants beside soft scrambled eggs and fresh juice make the café breakfast everyone orders on holiday mornings. It is simple, golden and endlessly comforting.

Ingredients

  • 4 croissants
  • 8 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1/4 cup cream
  • Orange juice, to serve

Directions

  1. Warm the croissants.
  2. Scramble the eggs gently in butter with cream.
  3. Serve with juice and flaky salt.

29. Strawberry Christmas Cocktails

Bright strawberry cocktails with a festive garnish bring a pop of red to the brunch table. Serve them in pretty glasses for an instant celebration.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups strawberry juice
  • 1 bottle sparkling wine
  • Fresh strawberries
  • Ice
  • Mint

Directions

  1. Chill the glasses and juice.
  2. Pour the juice over ice and top with sparkling wine.
  3. Garnish with strawberries and mint.

30. Cinnamon Hot Chocolate with Marshmallows

Thick, cinnamon-spiced hot chocolate under a cloud of marshmallows is the cozy drink Christmas mornings were made for. Make a big pot and let guests top their own.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups milk
  • 1/2 cup cocoa
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • Marshmallows
  • Whipped cream, optional

Directions

  1. Warm the milk with cocoa, sugar and cinnamon.
  2. Whisk until smooth and steaming.
  3. Pour into mugs and pile on marshmallows.

31. Poached Eggs with Smoked Salmon and Salad

Delicate poached eggs over smoked salmon with a light salad make a fresh, elegant plate that balances the richer brunch dishes. A lemon dressing keeps it bright.

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 8 oz smoked salmon
  • 4 cups salad leaves
  • 1 lemon
  • Olive oil
  • Dill

Directions

  1. Whisk the lemon juice and olive oil into a dressing.
  2. Poach the eggs for 3 minutes.
  3. Plate the salmon and salad, top with eggs and dressing.

32. Croissant with Jam and Fresh Fruit

A warm croissant, good jam and a handful of fresh fruit is the gentle, sweet start that suits slow Christmas mornings. Choose the ripest fruit you can find.

Ingredients

  • 4 croissants
  • Assorted jam
  • 2 cups fresh fruit
  • Butter

Directions

  1. Warm the croissants until crisp.
  2. Slice and spread with butter and jam.
  3. Serve with a pile of fresh fruit.

33. Sausage, Ham and Egg Breakfast Plate

Sausages, ham, a fried egg and toast make this hearty plate the answer for the biggest appetites at the table. Add a glass of cold milk and it is complete.

Ingredients

  • 4 sausages
  • 4 ham slices
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 toast slices
  • Butter
  • Milk, to serve

Directions

  1. Fry the sausages and ham until golden.
  2. Fry the eggs.
  3. Serve everything on warm plates with buttered toast.

34. Christmas Morning Breakfast Plate

Eggs, fresh fruit, toast and coffee on one vibrant plate is the classic Christmas morning breakfast, simple and full of color. It is the plate kids ask for first.

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 2 cups fresh fruit
  • 4 toast slices
  • Butter
  • Coffee

Directions

  1. Cook the eggs any style you like.
  2. Toast and butter the bread.
  3. Plate with fruit and serve with hot coffee.

35. Berry Citrus Cocktails

Iced berry and citrus cocktails on a wooden tray look like jewels and taste like a holiday toast. Make a pitcher so refills are easy.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups berry juice
  • 1 cup citrus juice
  • Sparkling water
  • Ice
  • Citrus slices
  • Berries

Directions

  1. Mix the berry and citrus juices.
  2. Pour over ice and top with sparkling water.
  3. Garnish with citrus slices and berries.

Christmas Brunch Tips

  • Make egg bakes, French toast bakes and pancake batter the night before so Christmas morning stays relaxed.
  • Keep the coffee and hot chocolate station self-serve so guests can refill while you finish the eggs.
  • Bake pastries and breads first, then cook eggs last — eggs wait for no one.
  • Set the table the night before with a festive runner, berries and fresh herbs for instant Christmas charm.

Final Thoughts

With these 35 Christmas brunch recipes, your holiday table will be full of warm, festive and crowd-pleasing dishes for every guest and every craving. Pick a few favorites, cook them with love, and enjoy the slowest, coziest morning of the season together.
