35+ Christmas Casseroles for a Crowd in One Dish

Oct 30, 2026

Nothing says Christmas dinner like a bubbling casserole pulled straight from the oven, and these Christmas casserole recipes are the coziest way to feed the people you love. From classic green bean casserole and cheesy potato bakes to festive lasagna, sausage stuffing bakes and make-ahead breakfast casseroles, this list covers every bubbling, golden-topped dish your holiday table needs.

Work through these 35 Christmas casserole recipes one weekend at a time, or pick your favorites for a Christmas dinner everyone remembers. Every recipe below comes with simple ingredients and easy directions, so even a first-time holiday host can fill the table with something warm and wonderful.

1. Classic Green Bean Casserole

Tender green beans folded into creamy mushroom sauce and crowned with crispy fried onions are the Christmas classic no holiday table is complete without. It is the side dish everyone asks for by name.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs green beans, trimmed
  • 2 cans cream of mushroom soup
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • 2 cups fried onions
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Blanch the green beans until just tender, then drain.
  2. Stir together the soup, milk, soy sauce and half the onions, then fold in the beans.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 25 minutes, top with the remaining onions and bake until golden.

2. Sweet Potato Casserole with Marshmallows

Whipped sweet potatoes with butter and brown sugar under a blanket of toasted marshmallows taste like dessert but count as a side. Kids and grown-ups fight over the gooey top.

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs sweet potatoes
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 2 cups mini marshmallows
  • Pinch of cinnamon

Directions

  1. Boil the sweet potatoes until tender, then drain and mash.
  2. Beat in the brown sugar, butter, milk, vanilla and cinnamon.
  3. Spread in a dish, top with marshmallows and bake until toasted.

3. Cheesy Hash Brown Potato Casserole

Crispy hash browns baked in a rich sour cream and cheddar sauce are pure comfort in a dish. This golden, cheesy bake disappears faster than any other side on the table.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs frozen hash browns, thawed
  • 2 cups cheddar, shredded
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 can cream of chicken soup
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 1/2 cup onion, diced
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Stir together the soup, sour cream, butter and onion.
  2. Fold in the hash browns and cheddar and season well.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 45 minutes until bubbling and golden.

4. Broccoli Cheddar Casserole

Bright broccoli florets smothered in a velvety cheddar sauce with a buttery crumb topping turn a simple vegetable into the star of the spread. Even broccoli skeptics go back for seconds.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs broccoli florets
  • 2 cups cheddar, shredded
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 1 1/2 cups milk
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Steam the broccoli until just tender and drain well.
  2. Make a roux with butter and flour, whisk in the milk, then melt in the cheddar.
  3. Pour over the broccoli, top with crumbs and bake until bubbling.

5. Sausage and Sage Stuffing Casserole

Savory sausage, fresh sage and buttery bread cubes baked until crisp on top and soft inside are everything you love about stuffing, in one easy dish. It tastes like Christmas morning smells.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pork sausage
  • 1 loaf day-old bread, cubed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tablespoon sage, chopped
  • 4 tablespoons butter

Directions

  1. Brown the sausage, then sauté the onion and celery in butter.
  2. Toss everything with the bread cubes, sage and stock.
  3. Bake covered at 350°F for 30 minutes, then uncovered until crisp.

6. Baked Holiday Macaroni and Cheese

Elbow pasta wrapped in a silky three-cheese sauce with a crunchy golden top is the ultimate crowd-pleaser. Make it the centerpiece for a cozy, kid-approved Christmas dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb elbow macaroni
  • 3 cups cheddar, shredded
  • 1 cup Gruyère, shredded
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 3 tablespoons flour
  • 3 cups milk
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs

Directions

  1. Cook the pasta until just shy of tender and drain.
  2. Make a cheese sauce with butter, flour, milk and both cheeses.
  3. Combine, top with crumbs and bake until golden and bubbling.

7. Creamy Chicken and Wild Rice Casserole

Tender chicken, nutty wild rice and mushrooms baked in a creamy sauce are comfort food at its most elegant. It is the make-ahead main that saves busy Christmas weeknights.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded
  • 2 cups cooked wild rice
  • 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 can cream of mushroom soup
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup mozzarella, shredded
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Stir together the chicken, rice, mushrooms, soup and sour cream.
  2. Spread in a buttered dish and top with mozzarella.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes until hot and bubbling.

8. Turkey Tetrazzini

Leftover turkey gets a glorious second act in this creamy pasta bake with mushrooms, peas and a golden parmesan crust. It is the best reason to cook a bigger bird.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked turkey, cubed
  • 12 oz spaghetti
  • 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 cup peas
  • 2 cups turkey stock
  • 1 cup cream
  • 1/2 cup parmesan, grated

Directions

  1. Cook the spaghetti until al dente and drain.
  2. Simmer the mushrooms in stock and cream, then toss with pasta, turkey and peas.
  3. Top with parmesan and bake until golden.

9. Ham and Cheddar Potato Casserole

Diced ham, tender potatoes and sharp cheddar baked in a creamy sauce make a hearty main that uses up holiday ham beautifully. One dish, zero leftovers wasted.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs potatoes, diced
  • 2 cups cooked ham, cubed
  • 2 cups cheddar, shredded
  • 1 can cream of potato soup
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1/2 cup onion, diced
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Parboil the potatoes until just tender and drain.
  2. Stir together the soup, sour cream and onion, then fold in potatoes, ham and cheese.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 40 minutes until bubbling.

10. Butternut Squash Casserole

Roasted butternut squash mashed with brown sugar, butter and a crunchy pecan topping tastes like autumn sunshine. It is the sweet-savory side that steals the show.

Ingredients

  • 2 butternut squash, cubed
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup pecans, chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • Salt

Directions

  1. Roast the squash until soft, then mash with butter and brown sugar.
  2. Season with cinnamon and nutmeg and spread in a dish.
  3. Top with pecans and bake until golden.

11. Cauliflower Gratin Casserole

Cauliflower florets baked in a rich Gruyère cream sauce with a crisp crumb top are so good nobody misses the carbs. This elegant gratin belongs on every festive table.

Ingredients

  • 1 large cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 1 cup Gruyère, grated
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Steam the cauliflower until just tender and drain well.
  2. Warm the cream with garlic, pour over the cauliflower and top with Gruyère.
  3. Add crumbs and bake until golden and bubbling.

12. Brussels Sprouts Gratin

Halved Brussels sprouts baked in cream with bacon and melted cheese turn the most debated vegetable into the most requested dish. The crispy cheesy top wins everyone over.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs Brussels sprouts, halved
  • 4 slices bacon, chopped
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup cheddar, shredded
  • 1/2 cup parmesan, grated
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Sauté the bacon, then toss the sprouts in the drippings.
  2. Pour over the cream and cheeses in a baking dish.
  3. Bake at 375°F for 30 minutes until golden.

13. Creamed Corn Casserole

Sweet corn baked into a soft, spoonable custard with butter and a hint of sugar is the old-fashioned side grandma always made. It pairs with ham, turkey and everything between.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups corn kernels
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 4 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Whisk the eggs, milk, butter, sugar and flour together.
  2. Stir in the corn and season well.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 40 minutes until set and golden.

14. Spinach and Artichoke Casserole

The beloved party dip becomes a bubbling baked side, with spinach, artichokes and three cheeses under a golden crust. Serve it straight from the dish with crusty bread.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs spinach, thawed and drained
  • 1 can artichoke hearts, chopped
  • 1 cup cream cheese
  • 1 cup mozzarella, shredded
  • 1/2 cup parmesan, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced

Directions

  1. Squeeze the spinach dry, then mix with artichokes, cheeses and garlic.
  2. Spread in a buttered dish and top with extra parmesan.
  3. Bake at 375°F for 25 minutes until bubbling.

15. Mushroom and Leek Casserole

Earthy mushrooms and silky leeks baked in a thyme-scented cream sauce are quietly luxurious. This is the side dish food lovers whisper about after dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 leeks, sliced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup Gruyère, grated
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Sauté the leeks and mushrooms in butter until golden.
  2. Stir in the cream and thyme and simmer briefly.
  3. Top with Gruyère and bake until bubbling.

16. Beef and Noodle Casserole

Seasoned ground beef, egg noodles and a tangy tomato-cheese sauce baked together are weeknight comfort dressed up for the holidays. Hearty, cheesy and endlessly satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 12 oz egg noodles
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 2 cups mozzarella, shredded
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning

Directions

  1. Brown the beef with onion, then stir in the marinara and seasoning.
  2. Cook the noodles, layer with beef sauce, sour cream and cheese.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes until bubbling.

17. Christmas Shepherd's Pie

Savory minced lamb and vegetables under a cloud of buttery mashed potatoes, with a festive twist of rosemary and parsnip, make a cozy Christmas main. A pie that hugs you back.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs ground lamb
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 2 lbs potatoes, mashed
  • 1 parsnip, mashed in
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste

Directions

  1. Brown the lamb with onion and carrots, then stir in tomato paste and stock.
  2. Simmer until thick, then spread in a dish.
  3. Top with the potato-parsnip mash and bake until golden.

18. Festive Lasagna

Layers of rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta and melted mozzarella, with a sprinkle of festive herbs, make lasagna the ultimate Christmas Eve main. It feeds a crowd and tastes even better the next day.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb Italian sausage
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 2 cups ricotta
  • 2 cups mozzarella, shredded
  • 1/2 cup parmesan, grated
  • 1 egg

Directions

  1. Brown the sausage and stir in the marinara.
  2. Mix the ricotta with egg and half the parmesan.
  3. Layer noodles, sauce, ricotta and mozzarella, then bake until bubbling.

19. Baked Ziti with Italian Sausage

Tube pasta, spicy sausage and bubbling mozzarella baked in marinara are the no-fuss Italian classic every Christmas gathering needs. Rustic, generous and always gone too fast.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ziti
  • 1 lb Italian sausage
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 2 cups mozzarella, shredded
  • 1 cup ricotta
  • 1/2 cup parmesan, grated
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning

Directions

  1. Cook the ziti until al dente and brown the sausage.
  2. Toss pasta with sausage, marinara and ricotta.
  3. Top with mozzarella and parmesan and bake until bubbling.

20. Chicken Pot Pie Casserole

All the cozy filling of chicken pot pie — tender chicken, carrots, peas and creamy gravy — baked under a flaky biscuit top in one big dish. No fiddly crust, all the comfort.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked chicken, cubed
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 cup peas
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 cup cream
  • 1 can refrigerated biscuits
  • 3 tablespoons flour

Directions

  1. Simmer the vegetables in stock, then whisk in flour and cream to thicken.
  2. Stir in the chicken and pour into a dish.
  3. Top with biscuits and bake until golden.

21. Cranberry Sweet Potato Casserole

Roasted sweet potatoes studded with tart cranberries and a brown sugar pecan crumble are the jewel-toned side your Christmas table has been missing. Sweet, tart and crunchy all at once.

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 1 cup fresh cranberries
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup pecans, chopped
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 1 teaspoon orange zest
  • Pinch of cinnamon

Directions

  1. Roast the sweet potatoes until tender.
  2. Toss with cranberries, orange zest and cinnamon in a dish.
  3. Top with brown sugar, pecans and butter and bake until bubbling.

22. French Onion Casserole

Caramelized onions, crusty bread and bubbling Gruyère baked together taste like French onion soup in casserole form. Rich, savory and impossibly cozy on a cold night.

Ingredients

  • 4 large onions, sliced
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 1 baguette, sliced
  • 2 cups Gruyère, shredded
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Caramelize the onions slowly in butter until deep golden.
  2. Layer bread, onions and cheese in a dish with stock and thyme.
  3. Bake at 375°F until bubbling and browned.

23. Loaded Baked Potato Casserole

Everything you love on a baked potato — bacon, cheese, sour cream and chives — baked into one glorious dish. It is the side that gets requested at every single gathering.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs potatoes, cubed
  • 6 slices bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • 2 cups cheddar, shredded
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Boil the potatoes until tender and drain.
  2. Toss with butter, sour cream, half the cheese and bacon.
  3. Top with remaining cheese and bake until golden.

24. Scalloped Potatoes

Paper-thin potatoes layered with cream, garlic and Gruyère and baked until golden are the most elegant side on the table. Every layer melts into the next.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/2 cups Gruyère, grated
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Butter a dish and layer the potatoes with garlic and cheese.
  2. Pour over the cream and season with nutmeg.
  3. Bake covered at 375°F for 45 minutes, then uncovered until golden.

25. Roasted Carrot and Parsnip Casserole

Honey-roasted carrots and parsnips baked with thyme and a touch of butter are sweet, earthy and gorgeously golden. A simple side that looks straight out of a magazine.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb carrots, sliced
  • 1 lb parsnips, sliced
  • 3 tablespoons honey
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 tablespoon thyme
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Toss the carrots and parsnips with honey, butter and thyme.
  2. Spread in a baking dish in a single layer.
  3. Roast at 400°F for 35 minutes until caramelized.

26. Eggplant Parmesan Casserole

Layers of tender eggplant, marinara and melted mozzarella baked until bubbling bring a taste of Italy to the Christmas table. Vegetarian, generous and deeply satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggplants, sliced
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 2 cups mozzarella, shredded
  • 1/2 cup parmesan, grated
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • Olive oil

Directions

  1. Dip the eggplant in egg and crumbs and pan-fry until golden.
  2. Layer with marinara and cheeses in a dish.
  3. Bake at 375°F for 30 minutes until bubbling.

27. Stuffed Shells Casserole

Jumbo shells stuffed with ricotta and spinach, nestled in marinara and baked under mozzarella, are little pockets of joy. Kids love them, adults love them more.

Ingredients

  • 20 jumbo pasta shells
  • 2 cups ricotta
  • 1 cup spinach, chopped
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 2 cups mozzarella, shredded
  • 1/2 cup parmesan, grated
  • 1 egg

Directions

  1. Cook the shells until al dente and drain.
  2. Mix the ricotta, spinach, egg and parmesan and fill each shell.
  3. Nestle in marinara, top with mozzarella and bake.

28. Roasted Vegetable Casserole

A rainbow of roasted peppers, zucchini, eggplant and red onion baked with herbs and feta is the bright, fresh counterpoint to a rich Christmas spread. Colorful and light.

Ingredients

  • 2 bell peppers, chopped
  • 2 zucchini, sliced
  • 1 eggplant, cubed
  • 1 red onion, wedged
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 cup feta, crumbled
  • 1 tablespoon Italian herbs

Directions

  1. Toss the vegetables with oil and herbs.
  2. Roast at 425°F for 30 minutes until charred at the edges.
  3. Transfer to a dish, top with feta and bake 10 minutes more.

29. Pumpkin and Sage Casserole

Silky pumpkin baked with crispy sage, cream and parmesan tastes like the holidays in a dish. It is unexpected, elegant and quietly addictive.

Ingredients

  • 1 sugar pumpkin, cubed
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup parmesan, grated
  • 12 sage leaves
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Roast the pumpkin until tender.
  2. Toss with cream, nutmeg and half the parmesan.
  3. Top with sage crisped in butter and bake until golden.

30. Apple Sausage Casserole

Sweet apples and savory sausage baked with stuffing cubes and sage are the sweet-savory surprise of the season. It tastes like a Christmas morning in the countryside.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pork sausage
  • 2 apples, diced
  • 1 loaf day-old bread, cubed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tablespoon sage
  • 4 tablespoons butter

Directions

  1. Brown the sausage, then sauté the onion and apples in butter.
  2. Toss with bread cubes, sage and stock.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 35 minutes until golden.

31. Cheesy Christmas Morning Strata

Cubed bread, eggs, cheese and a hint of mustard soaked overnight and baked golden make Christmas morning effortless. Assemble tonight, bake while the gifts are opened.

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf day-old bread, cubed
  • 8 eggs
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups cheddar, shredded
  • 1 teaspoon mustard
  • 1/2 cup onion, diced
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Whisk the eggs, milk, mustard and seasoning.
  2. Layer bread, cheese and onion in a dish and pour over the custard.
  3. Refrigerate overnight, then bake at 350°F for 45 minutes.

32. Overnight Breakfast Casserole

Sausage, eggs, hash browns and cheese layered and left to soak overnight bake into a hearty Christmas morning feast. Feed the whole family with one dish and zero morning stress.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb breakfast sausage
  • 2 cups frozen hash browns
  • 8 eggs
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups cheddar, shredded
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Brown the sausage and layer with hash browns, pepper and cheese.
  2. Whisk the eggs and milk and pour over.
  3. Refrigerate overnight, then bake at 350°F for 50 minutes.

33. Cinnamon Roll Casserole

Gooey cinnamon rolls baked with a vanilla custard and cream cheese drizzle are Christmas morning dessert disguised as breakfast. Nobody will believe how easy it was.

Ingredients

  • 2 cans cinnamon rolls, quartered
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/2 cup maple syrup
  • 4 oz cream cheese
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon

Directions

  1. Arrange the cinnamon roll pieces in a buttered dish.
  2. Whisk the eggs, milk, vanilla and cinnamon and pour over.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes and drizzle with cream cheese glaze.

34. Cornbread Chili Casserole

Spicy beef chili baked under a golden cornbread crust is the cozy, crowd-sized dish for a casual Christmas gathering. Scoop it straight from the dish with sour cream on top.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 can kidney beans
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 1 box cornbread mix
  • 1 cup cheddar, shredded
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1 onion, diced

Directions

  1. Brown the beef with onion, then stir in beans, tomatoes and chili powder.
  2. Spread in a dish and top with prepared cornbread batter.
  3. Bake at 375°F for 25 minutes until the cornbread is golden.

35. Cheesy Corn and Bacon Casserole

Sweet corn, smoky bacon and melted cheddar baked into a creamy, spoonable side are pure holiday comfort. It is the dish that disappears before the turkey is even carved.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups corn kernels
  • 6 slices bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • 1 cup cheddar, shredded
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Stir together the corn, sour cream, butter and flour.
  2. Fold in half the bacon and cheese.
  3. Top with the rest and bake at 350°F for 30 minutes until bubbling.

Christmas Casserole Recipes Tips

  • Assemble casseroles the day before and refrigerate; bake straight from the fridge with 10 extra minutes on the timer.
  • Let a casserole rest for 10 minutes before serving so it sets and slices cleanly.
  • Cover with foil for the first half of baking, then uncover to brown the top.
  • Double the recipe in two dishes when feeding a crowd — one for dinner, one for the freezer.

Final Thoughts

With these 35 Christmas casserole recipes, your holiday table will be full of warm, bubbling, crowd-pleasing dishes for every guest and every craving. Pick a few favorites, cook them with love, and enjoy the merriest dinner of the season together.
