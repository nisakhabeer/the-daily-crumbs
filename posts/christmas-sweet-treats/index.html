35+ Christmas Sweet Treats Everyone Will Love

Oct 26, 2026

When the tree is up and the house smells like cinnamon, only one thing belongs on the table: Christmas sweet treats. From spiced cookies and chocolate truffles to creamy trifles and festive cakes, these Christmas sweet treats cover every craving the season brings.

Work through this list of 35 sweet treats one bake at a time, or pick your favorites for a dessert table that guests will talk about long after the holidays. Every treat below comes with simple ingredients and easy directions, so even a first-time baker can fill the house with something wonderful.

1. Gingerbread Men Cookies

Soft, warmly spiced gingerbread men with piped icing smiles are the treat every Christmas table needs. Bake a big batch for decorating, gifting and nibbling straight from the tray.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 teaspoons ground ginger
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup molasses
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • Icing, to decorate

Directions

  1. Whisk the flour, baking soda, ginger and cinnamon together.
  2. Beat the butter, brown sugar, molasses and egg until smooth, then mix in the dry ingredients.
  3. Chill the dough 1 hour, roll out and cut into gingerbread men.
  4. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 9 to 11 minutes, cool completely, then decorate with icing.

2. Iced Christmas Cookies

Buttery cut-out cookies under swirls of bright royal icing turn an ordinary afternoon into a decorating party. Let everyone ice their own and no two cookies will ever match.

Ingredients

  • 2 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • Royal icing and sprinkles, to decorate

Directions

  1. Beat the butter and sugar until fluffy, then mix in the egg and vanilla.
  2. Stir in the flour, chill the dough 1 hour, then roll and cut into festive shapes.
  3. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 8 to 10 minutes until the edges are just golden.
  4. Cool completely, then ice and sprinkle as boldly as you like.

3. Christmas Cookie Gift Box

A box of mixed Christmas cookies is the gift that never misses — a little of everything, baked with love. Pack different shapes, flavors and colors so every bite is a surprise.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch gingerbread cookies
  • 1 batch sugar cookies
  • 1 batch chocolate cookies
  • Royal icing, to decorate
  • Sprinkles and edible glitter
  • Tissue paper and a sturdy box

Directions

  1. Bake three different cookie doughs and cool them completely.
  2. Decorate half the cookies with icing and sprinkles and leave the rest plain.
  3. Line a box with tissue paper and arrange the cookies in snug rows.
  4. Tuck in a handwritten tag and deliver within 3 days for the freshest taste.

4. Reindeer Sugar Pastries

These cute reindeer pastries, with pretzel antlers and a cherry-red nose, are made for kids and cameras alike. Flaky pastry and a sweet filling do all the talking.

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
  • 1/2 cup chocolate hazelnut spread
  • 24 mini pretzels
  • 24 red candies
  • 48 candy eyes
  • 1 egg, beaten

Directions

  1. Cut the pastry into 12 rounds and spread half with chocolate hazelnut spread.
  2. Top with the remaining rounds, seal the edges and brush with egg.
  3. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 12 to 14 minutes until puffed and golden.
  4. While warm, press in pretzel antlers, candy eyes and a red candy nose.

5. Candy Cane Cookies

Crisp, minty candy cane cookies striped in red and white look like they came straight off the tree. They are simple to shape and taste like a peppermint dream.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon peppermint extract
  • Red food coloring
  • Crushed candy canes, to top

Directions

  1. Beat the butter, sugar, egg and peppermint extract, then mix in the flour.
  2. Divide the dough in half and tint one half red.
  3. Roll red and white ropes together, twist into cane shapes and sprinkle with crushed candy.
  4. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 9 to 11 minutes until set but still pale.

6. Star Sugar Cookies

Golden star sugar cookies dusted with snow-like powdered sugar are simple, sweet and endlessly pretty. They stack beautifully on a dessert table or in a gift tin.

Ingredients

  • 2 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • Powdered sugar, to dust

Directions

  1. Beat the butter and sugar until light, then add the egg and vanilla.
  2. Mix in the flour, chill 1 hour, then roll and cut into stars.
  3. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 8 to 10 minutes until the edges turn golden.
  4. Cool and dust generously with powdered sugar before serving.

7. Chocolate Truffles

Rich, silky chocolate truffles with a soft ganache heart melt the moment they hit your tongue. Roll them in cocoa, nuts or sprinkles for a boxed-chocolate look at home.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Cocoa powder, for rolling
  • Chopped nuts or sprinkles, optional

Directions

  1. Heat the cream until steaming and pour over the chocolate and butter.
  2. Stir until smooth, then chill 2 hours until firm enough to scoop.
  3. Roll into small balls and coat in cocoa, nuts or sprinkles.
  4. Chill until set and serve at cool room temperature.

8. Assorted Chocolate Truffles

A tray of assorted truffles — dark, milk, white and nutty — makes any holiday table feel like a chocolate shop. Make several coatings from one ganache base.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz mixed chocolate, chopped
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Cocoa powder, crushed nuts and shredded coconut, for coating
  • Mini paper cups

Directions

  1. Make the ganache as for classic truffles and chill until scoopable.
  2. Roll into balls and divide into three bowls.
  3. Coat one batch in cocoa, one in nuts and one in coconut.
  4. Set each truffle in a mini paper cup and chill until serving.

9. Santa Hat Mini Cheesecakes

Tiny cheesecakes topped with strawberry Santa hats are the cutest bite on the dessert table. Creamy, fruity and festive, they vanish before the main desserts do.

Ingredients

  • 12 mini graham cracker crusts
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 12 strawberries, hulled
  • Whipped cream, to pipe

Directions

  1. Beat the cream cheese, sugar, egg and vanilla until smooth.
  2. Spoon into the mini crusts and bake at 325°F (165°C) for 15 to 18 minutes.
  3. Cool, then chill 2 hours until firm.
  4. Top each with a strawberry, point down, and pipe a whipped cream brim and pom-pom.

10. Mixed Berry Cheesecake

Creamy baked cheesecake piled with fresh mixed berries is a bright, beautiful finish to a rich Christmas dinner. The berries cut the richness perfectly.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 1/4 cup butter, melted
  • 24 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 2 cups mixed berries

Directions

  1. Press the crumbs and butter into a springform pan and chill.
  2. Beat the cream cheese, sugar, eggs and vanilla until silky.
  3. Pour over the crust and bake at 325°F (165°C) for 50 to 55 minutes.
  4. Cool, chill overnight, then pile high with mixed berries before serving.

11. Chocolate Cheesecake

Dark, dense and deeply chocolatey, this cheesecake is for the serious chocolate lovers at the table. A glossy ganache top makes it look bakery-bought.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups chocolate cookie crumbs
  • 1/4 cup butter, melted
  • 24 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 8 oz dark chocolate, melted
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/2 cup ganache, to top

Directions

  1. Press the crumbs and butter into a springform pan and chill.
  2. Beat the cream cheese and sugar, then blend in the melted chocolate and eggs.
  3. Bake at 325°F (165°C) for 55 to 60 minutes until just set.
  4. Cool, chill overnight and pour over the ganache before slicing.

12. Fudgy Chocolate Brownies

Dense, dark and unapologetically fudgy, these brownies are the base every Christmas dessert table needs. Cut them small — they are rich enough to satisfy.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter
  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 3/4 cup flour
  • 1/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla

Directions

  1. Melt the butter and chocolate together and cool slightly.
  2. Whisk in the sugar, eggs and vanilla, then fold in the flour and cocoa.
  3. Spread in a lined pan and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 22 to 25 minutes.
  4. Cool completely before cutting into neat squares.

13. Salted Caramel Brownies

Fudgy brownies swirled with salted caramel are sweet, salty and impossible to leave alone. The caramel sinks into every crack for a gooey center.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch brownie batter
  • 1/2 cup caramel sauce
  • 1 teaspoon flaky sea salt
  • 1/4 cup chocolate chips

Directions

  1. Prepare the brownie batter and spread half in a lined pan.
  2. Drizzle with half the caramel and sprinkle with salt, then top with the rest of the batter.
  3. Swirl the remaining caramel over the top with a knife.
  4. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 24 to 27 minutes and cool before cutting.

14. Festive Hot Chocolate

Thick, glossy hot chocolate crowned with marshmallows is the coziest sweet treat of all. Serve it in big mugs with a sparkler for a party moment.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups whole milk
  • 6 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • Marshmallows, to top
  • Whipped cream, optional

Directions

  1. Warm the milk until steaming, then whisk in the cocoa and sugar.
  2. Add the chopped chocolate and stir until melted and glossy.
  3. Simmer 2 minutes, then pour into mugs.
  4. Top with marshmallows and whipped cream and serve hot.

15. Cozy Marshmallow Hot Chocolate

A blanket, a fire and a mug of marshmallow-topped hot chocolate is Christmas in a cup. Use real chocolate for a drink that tastes like dessert.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups milk
  • 5 oz milk chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1 cup mini marshmallows

Directions

  1. Heat the milk with the cocoa until steaming.
  2. Stir in the chocolate until melted, then add the vanilla.
  3. Pour into mugs and pile on the mini marshmallows.
  4. Serve right away while the marshmallows are still soft.

16. Peppermint Hot Chocolate

Cool peppermint whipped cream over rich cocoa, with a candy cane for stirring, tastes like the holidays in a mug. It is the easiest festive upgrade there is.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups milk
  • 6 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon peppermint extract
  • Whipped cream, to top
  • 2 candy canes, crushed
  • Chocolate syrup, to drizzle

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and melt in the chocolate until smooth.
  2. Stir in the peppermint extract.
  3. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream.
  4. Drizzle with chocolate syrup and scatter with crushed candy cane.

17. Candy Cane Ice Cream

Peppermint ice cream with crushed candy cane crunch is a cold, minty treat that balances a table full of baked goods. Scoop it into cones or festive bowls.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon peppermint extract
  • 1/2 cup crushed candy canes
  • Cones, to serve

Directions

  1. Whisk the cream, milk, sugar and peppermint extract together.
  2. Churn in an ice cream maker according to the maker's directions.
  3. Fold in the crushed candy canes in the last 5 minutes.
  4. Freeze until firm and scoop into cones.

18. Christmas Fruitcake

Dark, fruity and soaked with festive spirit, a good Christmas fruitcake only gets better with time. Make it early and let the flavors deepen before the big day.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed dried fruit
  • 1 cup chopped nuts
  • 1 3/4 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon mixed spice
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/4 cup brandy or juice

Directions

  1. Soak the fruit and nuts in brandy or juice overnight.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar, then add the eggs one at a time.
  3. Fold in the flour, spice and soaked fruit.
  4. Bake in a lined loaf pan at 300°F (150°C) for 1 1/2 to 2 hours, then wrap and rest.

19. Panettone

Tall, golden and studded with candied fruit, panettone is Italy's beloved Christmas bread. Slice it thick and serve with coffee or a cloud of whipped cream.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups bread flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 teaspoons yeast
  • 1/2 cup warm milk
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup candied fruit and raisins
  • Zest of 1 orange

Directions

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, yeast and warm milk, then knead in the butter and eggs.
  2. Knead in the fruit and zest, then let rise until doubled.
  3. Shape into a tall round and place in a panettone mold.
  4. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 35 to 40 minutes until deep golden.

20. Gingerbread Cake

Warm gingerbread cake with a snowy icing top is easier than gingerbread cookies and feeds a crowd. Serve it warm with cream for the coziest dessert of the season.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 teaspoons ginger
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup molasses
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup hot water
  • Icing and sprinkles, to top

Directions

  1. Whisk the flour, soda and spices together.
  2. Melt the butter with the molasses and sugar, then beat in the egg.
  3. Stir in the dry ingredients and hot water until smooth.
  4. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 35 to 40 minutes, cool, then ice and sprinkle.

21. Chocolate Bundt Cake

A rich chocolate bundt with a shiny glaze looks stunning with almost no decorating effort. It slices beautifully and feeds a whole party.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 3/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • Chocolate glaze, to drizzle

Directions

  1. Beat the butter and sugar, then add the eggs and vanilla.
  2. Mix in the flour, cocoa and sour cream until smooth.
  3. Pour into a greased bundt pan and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 50 to 55 minutes.
  4. Cool, turn out and drizzle with chocolate glaze.

22. Chocolate Christmas Cake

Layers of moist chocolate cake under festive decorations are the centerpiece every Christmas table deserves. Dress it with berries, rosemary and a dusting of sugar snow.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 3/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • Chocolate frosting
  • Berries and rosemary, to decorate

Directions

  1. Beat the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then mix in the flour, cocoa and milk.
  2. Divide between two lined pans and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 30 to 35 minutes.
  3. Cool completely, then stack with chocolate frosting.
  4. Frost the outside and decorate with berries and rosemary.

23. Christmas Tree Cake

A cake shaped and iced like a Christmas tree is pure holiday magic, especially for kids. Green frosting, sprinkle ornaments and a star on top finish the look.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch vanilla cake batter
  • Green buttercream frosting
  • Assorted sprinkles
  • 1 star topper
  • Powdered sugar, to dust

Directions

  1. Bake the cake in a round pan and cool completely.
  2. Cut and stack the layers into a tree shape, trimming as needed.
  3. Frost all over with green buttercream.
  4. Decorate with sprinkles, dust with sugar snow and crown with the star.

24. Strawberry Christmas Trifle

Layers of sponge, custard, cream and strawberries in a glass bowl show off every pretty layer. It is the make-ahead dessert that looks like you tried for hours.

Ingredients

  • 1 sponge cake, cubed
  • 2 cups custard
  • 2 cups whipped cream
  • 2 cups strawberries, sliced
  • 1/4 cup strawberry jam
  • Shaved chocolate, to top

Directions

  1. Spread the jam over the sponge cubes.
  2. Layer sponge, custard, cream and strawberries in a glass bowl.
  3. Repeat the layers until the bowl is full.
  4. Chill 4 hours, then top with shaved chocolate before serving.

25. Chocolate Strawberry Trifle

Chocolate cake, chocolate custard and fresh strawberries layer into a trifle that tastes like a celebration. It is rich, fruity and made for a crowd.

Ingredients

  • 1 chocolate cake, cubed
  • 2 cups chocolate custard
  • 2 cups whipped cream
  • 2 cups strawberries, sliced
  • Chocolate shavings, to top

Directions

  1. Layer chocolate cake cubes in a glass bowl.
  2. Spoon over chocolate custard, then whipped cream and strawberries.
  3. Repeat the layers until the bowl is full.
  4. Chill 4 hours and finish with chocolate shavings.

26. Berry Dessert Parfaits

Individual berry parfaits layer cake, cream and a tumble of fresh berries in every glass. They are light, pretty and easy to scale for any size of party.

Ingredients

  • 1 sponge cake, cubed
  • 2 cups whipped cream
  • 2 cups mixed berries
  • 1/4 cup berry jam
  • Mint leaves, to garnish

Directions

  1. Stir the jam through half the berries to make a quick compote.
  2. Layer sponge, cream, compote and fresh berries in glasses.
  3. Repeat until the glasses are full.
  4. Chill 2 hours and garnish with mint before serving.

27. Berry Pavlova Wreath

Crisp meringue, soft cream and a ring of fresh berries shaped into a wreath is the showstopper of the dessert table. It is light after a heavy Christmas dinner.

Ingredients

  • 4 egg whites
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon cornstarch
  • 1 1/2 cups whipped cream
  • 2 cups mixed berries
  • Mint leaves, to garnish

Directions

  1. Whisk the egg whites until soft peaks, then beat in the sugar until glossy.
  2. Fold in the vinegar and cornstarch and shape into a ring on a lined sheet.
  3. Bake at 250°F (120°C) for 1 1/2 hours, then cool in the oven.
  4. Top with whipped cream, berries and mint just before serving.

28. Mini Berry Pavlovas

Little meringue nests filled with cream and berries let everyone have their own perfect pavlova. They are crisp, creamy and impossibly pretty.

Ingredients

  • 3 egg whites
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vinegar
  • 1 cup whipped cream
  • 1 1/2 cups berries

Directions

  1. Whisk the egg whites and sugar until stiff and glossy, then fold in the vinegar.
  2. Spoon into 12 nests on a lined sheet.
  3. Bake at 250°F (120°C) for 1 hour, then cool in the oven.
  4. Fill with whipped cream and berries just before serving.

29. Lattice Apple Pie

A golden lattice apple pie with cinnamon-spiced apples is the holiday classic that never goes out of style. Serve it warm with cream or ice cream.

Ingredients

  • 2 pie crusts
  • 6 apples, sliced
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 tablespoon flour
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 egg, beaten

Directions

  1. Toss the apples with sugar, cinnamon and flour.
  2. Line a pie dish with one crust and fill with the apples.
  3. Weave the second crust into a lattice over the top and brush with egg.
  4. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 45 to 50 minutes until deep golden.

30. Apple Crumble Tart

Buttery crumble over tender spiced apples in a crisp tart shell is comfort in dessert form. It is simpler than pie and just as welcome.

Ingredients

  • 1 tart shell, baked
  • 4 apples, sliced
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 3/4 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup oats
  • 1/2 cup butter, cold and cubed

Directions

  1. Toss the apples with half the sugar and the cinnamon and fill the tart shell.
  2. Rub the flour, oats, remaining sugar and butter into a crumble.
  3. Scatter the crumble thickly over the apples.
  4. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 30 to 35 minutes until golden and bubbling.

31. Christmas Donuts

Glazed donuts dressed for Christmas with sprinkles and festive colors are a fun, easy sweet for any holiday morning. Kids love decorating them most of all.

Ingredients

  • 12 plain glazed donuts
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 tablespoons milk
  • Red and green sprinkles
  • Crushed candy canes

Directions

  1. Whisk the powdered sugar and milk into a thick glaze.
  2. Dip the top of each donut in the glaze.
  3. Sprinkle with red and green sprinkles and crushed candy cane.
  4. Let set 20 minutes before serving.

32. Chocolate Mousse Cups

Silky chocolate mousse spooned into cups with strawberries and cream is an elegant no-bake dessert. Make it ahead and let the fridge do the work.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream, divided
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • Strawberries and whipped cream, to top

Directions

  1. Melt the chocolate with 1/2 cup of the cream and cool slightly.
  2. Whip the remaining cream with the sugar and vanilla until soft peaks.
  3. Fold the chocolate into the whipped cream until smooth.
  4. Spoon into cups, chill 2 hours and top with strawberries and cream.

33. Raspberry Chocolate Mousse

Dark chocolate mousse with a tart raspberry on top is a small, luxurious bite that ends dinner on a high note. A few gold pearls make it party-ready.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream, divided
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 cup raspberries
  • Gold sprinkles, to top

Directions

  1. Melt the chocolate with 1/2 cup of the cream and cool slightly.
  2. Whip the remaining cream and sugar until soft peaks, then fold in the chocolate.
  3. Spoon into small glasses and chill 2 hours.
  4. Top each with a raspberry and a few gold sprinkles.

34. Classic Tiramisu

Coffee-soaked ladyfingers under clouds of mascarpone cream, dusted with cocoa, make the most-loved Italian dessert. It only gets better overnight.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups mascarpone
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 cup strong coffee, cooled
  • 24 ladyfingers
  • Cocoa powder, to dust

Directions

  1. Beat the mascarpone, cream and sugar until thick and smooth.
  2. Dip each ladyfinger briefly in the coffee and layer in a dish.
  3. Spread over half the cream, repeat the layers and smooth the top.
  4. Chill overnight and dust generously with cocoa before serving.

35. Tiramisu Cups

Individual tiramisu cups layer coffee-soaked sponge and mascarpone cream into perfect single servings. They look elegant and need no slicing at all.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mascarpone
  • 1 cup whipped cream
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup strong coffee, cooled
  • 12 ladyfingers, broken
  • Cocoa powder, to dust

Directions

  1. Beat the mascarpone, whipped cream and sugar until smooth.
  2. Dip the ladyfinger pieces briefly in the coffee.
  3. Layer coffee-soaked pieces and cream in small glasses.
  4. Chill 4 hours and dust with cocoa before serving.

Sweet Treats Tips

  • Bake cookies and cakes ahead and freeze them undecorated, then ice and finish on the day.
  • Chill trifles, mousses and tiramisu overnight — they taste better the next day.
  • Label gift boxes with the treat name and the date baked so nothing goes stale.
  • Keep a mix of baked, no-bake and chilled treats so the oven never has to do everything at once.

Final Thoughts

With these 35 Christmas sweet treats, your holiday table will be full of cookies, cakes, chocolates and creamy desserts for every guest and every craving. Pick a few favorites, bake them with love, and enjoy the sweetest part of the season together.
