35+ Christmas Hot Chocolate Recipes for Cozy Winter Nights

Oct 30, 2026

Nothing warms up a Christmas night like a mug of rich, creamy hot chocolate, and these Christmas hot chocolate recipes cover every craving your holiday season can dream up. From the classic stovetop cocoa of childhood to peppermint, salted caramel, Mexican spiced and even frozen hot chocolate, this list has a cozy mug for every person on your list.

Work through these 35 Christmas hot chocolate recipes one snowy evening at a time, or set up a cocoa bar and let everyone build their own. Every recipe below comes with simple ingredients and easy directions, so you can stir, sip and savor the sweetest part of the season.

1. Classic Creamy Hot Chocolate

Real milk chocolate, warm milk and a cloud of whipped cream make the classic hot chocolate every Christmas needs. It is simple, silky and exactly like the cocoa you grew up with, only better.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup milk chocolate chips
  • 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
  • Whipped cream, for topping

Directions

  1. Warm the milk in a saucepan over medium heat until steaming, not boiling.
  2. Whisk in the chocolate chips, cocoa and sugar until completely smooth.
  3. Stir in the vanilla, pour into mugs and top with whipped cream.

2. Peppermint Hot Chocolate

Cool peppermint and rich chocolate with a crushed candy cane rim taste like Christmas in a mug. It is festive, refreshing and the first cocoa everyone asks for at a holiday party.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup dark chocolate chips
  • 1/2 teaspoon peppermint extract
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Crushed candy canes, for topping
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm the milk until steaming, then whisk in the chocolate and sugar until smooth.
  2. Stir in the peppermint extract, a little at a time so it stays balanced.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and a shower of crushed candy cane.

3. Salted Caramel Hot Chocolate

Buttery caramel and a pinch of flaky salt turn ordinary cocoa into a salted caramel dream. Sweet, salty and impossibly cozy, it is the mug you make twice in one night.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup milk chocolate chips
  • 3 tablespoons caramel sauce, plus more for drizzling
  • 1/4 teaspoon flaky sea salt
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and whisk in the chocolate until smooth.
  2. Stir in the caramel sauce and sea salt.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and an extra caramel drizzle.

4. White Chocolate Hot Chocolate

Sweet white chocolate and vanilla make a snowy, creamy cocoa that feels like a winter wonderland. It is gentle, dreamy and perfect for anyone who finds dark chocolate too intense.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 3/4 cup white chocolate chips
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1 tablespoon sugar (optional)
  • Whipped cream
  • White chocolate shavings, for topping

Directions

  1. Warm the milk until steaming, then whisk in the white chocolate until melted.
  2. Stir in the vanilla and sugar if using.
  3. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream and white chocolate shavings.

5. Dark Chocolate Truffle Hot Chocolate

Intense dark chocolate and a splash of cream make a thick, truffle-like cocoa for serious chocolate lovers. It drinks like a melted truffle and warms you from the inside out.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 4 ounces dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • Chocolate curls, for topping

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and cream together until steaming.
  2. Whisk in the dark chocolate, cocoa and sugar until thick and glossy.
  3. Pour into small mugs and top with chocolate curls.

6. Mexican Spiced Hot Chocolate

Cinnamon, a pinch of chili and rich chocolate make a Mexican spiced cocoa with a warm, slow heat. It is bold, ancient and the most interesting mug on the whole list.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 3 ounces dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 pinch chili powder
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla

Directions

  1. Warm the milk with the cinnamon and chili until steaming.
  2. Whisk in the chocolate and sugar until smooth.
  3. Stir in the vanilla and pour into mugs, dusting with extra cinnamon.

7. Nutella Hazelnut Hot Chocolate

Chocolate hazelnut spread whisked into warm milk makes a Nutella cocoa that tastes like dessert in a mug. Nutty, sweet and dangerously drinkable, it disappears fast.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/3 cup chocolate hazelnut spread
  • 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
  • Chopped hazelnuts, for topping
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm the milk until steaming, then whisk in the hazelnut spread and cocoa until smooth.
  2. Stir in the vanilla.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and chopped hazelnuts.

8. Coconut Hot Chocolate

Creamy coconut milk and chocolate with toasted coconut on top make a tropical twist on Christmas cocoa. It is silky, snowy and surprisingly perfect by the fire.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1/2 cup milk chocolate chips
  • 1/2 teaspoon coconut extract
  • Toasted coconut flakes, for topping
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm both milks together until steaming.
  2. Whisk in the chocolate until smooth, then stir in the coconut extract.
  3. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream and toasted coconut.

9. Gingerbread Hot Chocolate

Ginger, molasses and warm spices turn cocoa into liquid gingerbread. Serve it with a mini gingerbread man on the rim for the cutest mug of the season.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup milk chocolate chips
  • 1 tablespoon molasses
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 pinch nutmeg
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm the milk with the ginger, cinnamon and nutmeg until steaming.
  2. Whisk in the chocolate and molasses until smooth.
  3. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream and a gingerbread cookie.

10. Eggnog Hot Chocolate

Two Christmas classics in one mug: rich eggnog and silky chocolate with a dusting of nutmeg. It is creamy, nostalgic and tastes like the holidays themselves.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup eggnog
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1/2 cup milk chocolate chips
  • 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm the eggnog and milk together until steaming, taking care not to boil.
  2. Whisk in the chocolate until smooth, then stir in the vanilla.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and a dusting of nutmeg.

11. Candy Cane Hot Chocolate

Crushed candy canes melted right into the cocoa make a sweet peppermint mug with a pretty pink swirl. It is the easiest way to use up the candy canes on the tree.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup white chocolate chips
  • 3 candy canes, crushed
  • 1/2 teaspoon peppermint extract
  • Whipped cream
  • 1 mini candy cane, for stirring

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and whisk in the white chocolate until smooth.
  2. Stir in half the crushed candy canes and the peppermint extract.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream, the rest of the crushed candy and a mini candy cane.

12. Toasted Marshmallow Hot Chocolate

A campfire in a mug: classic cocoa piled with marshmallows toasted golden under the broiler. The gooey, toasty top is half the fun of drinking it.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup milk chocolate chips
  • 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Mini marshmallows, for topping

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and whisk in the chocolate, cocoa and sugar until smooth.
  2. Pour into oven-safe mugs and pile on mini marshmallows.
  3. Broil for 1 minute until golden and toasted, then serve immediately.

13. Peanut Butter Hot Chocolate

Creamy peanut butter whisked into chocolate makes a peanut butter cup you can drink. Rich, salty-sweet and completely addictive, it is a grown-up treat kids love too.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup milk chocolate chips
  • 3 tablespoons creamy peanut butter
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Chopped peanuts, for topping
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and whisk in the chocolate and peanut butter until completely smooth.
  2. Stir in the sugar.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and chopped peanuts.

14. Mocha Hot Chocolate

A shot of espresso deepens the chocolate into a mocha that wakes you up and warms you up at once. It is the morning-after-Christmas mug, or any cold afternoon.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup brewed espresso or strong coffee
  • 1/2 cup dark chocolate chips
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Whipped cream
  • Cocoa powder, for dusting

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and whisk in the chocolate and sugar until smooth.
  2. Stir in the espresso.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and a dusting of cocoa.

15. Spiced Chai Hot Chocolate

Chai spices — cardamom, ginger, cinnamon — steeped into cocoa make a fragrant, warming mug that smells like a spice market at Christmas. It is cozy with a little mystery.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 2 chai tea bags
  • 1/2 cup milk chocolate chips
  • 1/4 teaspoon cardamom
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm the milk with the chai tea bags and cardamom for 5 minutes, then remove the bags.
  2. Whisk in the chocolate and sugar until smooth.
  3. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream and a pinch of cinnamon.

16. Orange Hot Chocolate

Bright orange zest and juice lift dark chocolate into a citrusy cocoa that tastes like a chocolate orange from a stocking. It is sunny, rich and wonderfully festive.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup dark chocolate chips
  • 1 tablespoon orange zest
  • 2 tablespoons orange juice
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Orange slices, for garnish

Directions

  1. Warm the milk with the orange zest until steaming.
  2. Whisk in the chocolate and sugar until smooth, then stir in the orange juice.
  3. Pour into mugs and garnish with an orange slice.

17. Raspberry Hot Chocolate

Tart raspberries swirled through chocolate make a jewel-red cocoa that is as pretty as it is delicious. Fresh berries on top make it party-ready in seconds.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup dark chocolate chips
  • 1/2 cup fresh raspberries, plus more for topping
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and whisk in the chocolate and sugar until smooth.
  2. Mash in the raspberries and strain out the seeds if you like.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and fresh raspberries.

18. Mint Chocolate Chip Hot Chocolate

Cool mint, green-tinted cream and mini chocolate chips turn cocoa into a mint chip ice cream you can drink warm. It is playful, pastel and a hit with kids.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup white chocolate chips
  • 1/2 teaspoon mint extract
  • 2 tablespoons mini chocolate chips
  • 1 drop green food coloring (optional)
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and whisk in the white chocolate until smooth.
  2. Stir in the mint extract and food coloring if using.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and a sprinkle of mini chocolate chips.

19. Cookies and Cream Hot Chocolate

Crushed chocolate sandwich cookies stirred into white chocolate cocoa make a cookies-and-cream mug that tastes like dunking without the dunking. The cookie crumbs on top are the best part.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 3/4 cup white chocolate chips
  • 4 chocolate sandwich cookies, crushed
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and whisk in the white chocolate until smooth.
  2. Stir in half the crushed cookies and the vanilla.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and the remaining cookie crumbs.

20. S'mores Hot Chocolate

Graham cracker crumbs, toasted marshmallow and chocolate make a campfire s'more in a mug — no fire required. It is the coziest possible way to end a winter night.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup milk chocolate chips
  • 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
  • Graham cracker crumbs, for topping
  • Mini marshmallows, for topping
  • Chocolate syrup, for drizzling

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and whisk in the chocolate and cocoa until smooth.
  2. Pour into mugs and top with marshmallows.
  3. Toast the marshmallows with a kitchen torch or under the broiler, then sprinkle with graham crumbs and drizzle with chocolate syrup.

21. Frozen Hot Chocolate

Blended chocolate, milk and ice make a frosty frozen hot chocolate that tastes like a chocolate milkshake dressed for Christmas. It is the mug for warm-house parties and kids who want in on the cocoa fun.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups ice
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1/3 cup chocolate syrup
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Blend the ice, milk, chocolate syrup, cocoa and sugar until thick and slushy.
  2. Pour into tall glasses.
  3. Top with whipped cream and an extra drizzle of chocolate syrup.

22. Slow Cooker Hot Chocolate

The party hero: chocolate, milk and cream melting together in the slow cooker while you decorate the tree. Guests ladle their own, and the cocoa stays warm all night.

Ingredients

  • 6 cups whole milk
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 2 cups milk chocolate chips
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla

Directions

  1. Combine everything except the vanilla in the slow cooker.
  2. Cook on low for 2 hours, whisking every 30 minutes until smooth.
  3. Stir in the vanilla, set to warm, and let guests ladle their own with toppings on the side.

23. Hot Chocolate Bombs

Chocolate spheres packed with cocoa and marshmallows that burst open in hot milk — the most magical mug on the list. Make a batch for gifts and one for yourself.

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces chocolate candy melts
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 cup mini marshmallows
  • Sprinkles, for decorating
  • Hot milk, for serving

Directions

  1. Melt the candy melts and coat silicone sphere molds, then chill until firm.
  2. Fill half the spheres with cocoa and marshmallows, then seal with a warm empty half.
  3. Decorate with drizzled chocolate and sprinkles, then drop into hot milk and watch them burst.

24. Red Velvet Hot Chocolate

Red velvet cake in a mug: cocoa, a hint of vanilla and cream cheese whipped cream on top. It is dramatic, delicious and the prettiest red on the Christmas table.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup milk chocolate chips
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • Red food coloring, a few drops
  • Cream cheese whipped cream, for topping

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and whisk in the chocolate, cocoa and vanilla until smooth.
  2. Tint with red food coloring until velvety red.
  3. Pour into mugs and top with cream cheese whipped cream.

25. Pumpkin Spice Hot Chocolate

Pumpkin puree and pumpkin spice whisked into cocoa make the fall favorite all dressed up for Christmas. It is autumn and winter shaking hands in one warm mug.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup milk chocolate chips
  • 1/4 cup pumpkin puree
  • 1 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm the milk with the pumpkin puree and spice until steaming.
  2. Whisk in the chocolate and sugar until smooth.
  3. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream and a pinch of pumpkin spice.

26. Maple Hot Chocolate

Real maple syrup sweetens this cocoa with a woodsy, caramel warmth that tastes like a cabin in December. It is simple, old-fashioned and quietly perfect.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup milk chocolate chips
  • 3 tablespoons pure maple syrup
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
  • Whipped cream
  • Maple syrup, for drizzling

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and whisk in the chocolate until smooth.
  2. Stir in the maple syrup and vanilla.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and a thin maple drizzle.

27. Almond Joy Hot Chocolate

Almond, coconut and chocolate — the candy bar, melted into a mug. Toasted almonds and coconut on top make it taste exactly like the bar you love.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup milk chocolate chips
  • 1/2 teaspoon almond extract

  • Sliced almonds, for topping

Directions

  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream, sliced almonds and extra coconut.

28. Caramel Apple Hot Chocolate

Apple cider, caramel and chocolate meet in a mug that tastes like a caramel apple dipped in cocoa. It is orchard-sweet, festive and made for cold December nights.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup apple cider
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1/2 cup milk chocolate chips
  • 3 tablespoons caramel sauce
  • Apple slices, for garnish
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm the cider and milk together until steaming.
  2. Whisk in the chocolate and caramel until smooth.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and an apple slice.

29. Cherry Hot Chocolate

Dark cherries and chocolate are a classic pair, and this cocoa proves it with every sip. Tart, sweet and deep red, it is a Black Forest cake you can drink.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup dark chocolate chips
  • 1/2 cup cherry juice or cherry syrup
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Fresh or maraschino cherries, for topping
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and whisk in the chocolate and sugar until smooth.
  2. Stir in the cherry juice.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and a cherry.

30. Lavender White Hot Chocolate

A whisper of lavender steeped into white chocolate cocoa makes a floral, calming mug unlike any other on the list. It is elegant, unusual and lovely for a quiet Christmas evening.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 teaspoon culinary dried lavender
  • 3/4 cup white chocolate chips
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • Whipped cream
  • Lavender buds, for garnish

Directions

  1. Warm the milk with the lavender for 5 minutes, then strain out the buds.
  2. Whisk in the white chocolate and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and a few lavender buds.

31. Espresso Hot Chocolate

Double espresso meets dark chocolate in a bold, grown-up cocoa that doubles as dessert and a pick-me-up. It is the mug for late-night wrapping sessions.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1 cup brewed espresso
  • 1/2 cup dark chocolate chips
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Whipped cream
  • Chocolate-covered espresso beans, for topping

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and whisk in the chocolate and sugar until smooth.
  2. Stir in the espresso.
  3. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream and chocolate-covered espresso beans.

32. Hot Chocolate Float

Hot cocoa poured over vanilla ice cream makes a float that is half drink, half dessert, all joy. The melting ice cream swirls into the chocolate as you sip.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup milk chocolate chips
  • 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
  • 2 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • Chocolate syrup, for drizzling

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and whisk in the chocolate and cocoa until smooth.
  2. Place a scoop of ice cream in each tall glass.
  3. Pour the hot cocoa over the ice cream and drizzle with chocolate syrup.

33. Spicy Chili Hot Chocolate

Cayenne and cinnamon give this cocoa a slow, smoky burn that builds with every sip. It is not for the faint of heart — it is for the ones who like their Christmas with a kick.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup dark chocolate chips
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm the milk with the cayenne and cinnamon until steaming.
  2. Whisk in the chocolate and sugar until smooth.
  3. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream and a pinch of cayenne.

34. Hot Chocolate Bar

Not one mug but a whole party: a big pot of cocoa with a bar of toppings so every guest builds their dream cup. Marshmallows, candy canes, caramel and sprinkles — the bar is the celebration.

Ingredients

  • 6 cups whole milk
  • 2 cups milk chocolate chips
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • Mini marshmallows, crushed candy canes, caramel sauce, sprinkles, whipped cream

Directions

  1. Warm the milk and whisk in the chocolate, cocoa and sugar until smooth.
  2. Pour into a slow cooker set to warm or a large pot on low.
  3. Set out bowls of toppings and let guests build their own mugs.

35. Whipped Hot Chocolate

Fluffy whipped cocoa cream spooned over cold milk makes the viral whipped hot chocolate — light, chocolatey and impossibly photogenic. Stir it in and watch it turn into the creamiest cocoa ever.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 2 tablespoons powdered sugar
  • 1 cup cold milk
  • Ice
  • Chocolate shavings, for topping

Directions

  1. Whip the cream, cocoa and powdered sugar until stiff, fluffy peaks form.
  2. Fill glasses with cold milk and ice.
  3. Spoon the whipped cocoa over the top, dust with chocolate shavings and stir to drink.

Christmas Hot Chocolate Tips

  • Warm milk slowly and never let it boil, or the chocolate can turn grainy and the milk can scorch.
  • Use real chopped chocolate or good chocolate chips instead of cocoa alone for the silkiest, richest cocoa.
  • Make the base ahead in a slow cooker for parties, then set out toppings so guests can build their own mugs.
  • Add extracts, spices and liqueur-style flavors at the very end so their aroma stays bright.
  • Top with whipped cream, marshmallows or a drizzle right before serving so every mug looks its best.

Final Thoughts

With these 35 Christmas hot chocolate recipes, your holiday season will be full of warm, cozy, chocolatey mugs for every craving and every cold night. Pick a few favorites, stir them with love, and enjoy the sweetest sips of the season together.
