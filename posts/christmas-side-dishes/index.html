35+ Christmas Side Dishes That Outshine the Main Course

Oct 30, 2026

Christmas dinner deserves sides that shine just as brightly as the main dish, and these Christmas side dishes are exactly how you get there. From creamy mashed potatoes and classic herb stuffing to honey glazed carrots, crispy Brussels sprouts and golden casseroles, this list covers every dish your holiday table needs.

Work through these 35 Christmas side dishes one weekend at a time, or pick your favorites for a Christmas dinner everyone remembers. Every recipe below comes with simple ingredients and easy directions, so even a first-time holiday host can fill the table with something wonderful.

1. Creamy Pumpkin Mashed Potatoes

Silky mashed potatoes swirled with sweet pumpkin and warm spices are the cozy twist your Christmas table has been waiting for. They taste like fall in a bowl and pair with absolutely everything.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 1 cup pumpkin puree
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup warm milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, then drain well.
  2. Mash with the butter, warm milk and pumpkin puree until smooth.
  3. Season with cinnamon, nutmeg, salt and pepper and serve warm.

2. Classic Herb Stuffing

Buttery bread cubes, sautéed onions, celery and a handful of fresh herbs make this the stuffing everyone reaches for first. Bake it golden on top and soft in the middle.

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf day-old bread, cubed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cups vegetable stock
  • 1 tablespoon sage, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon thyme
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Sauté the onion and celery in butter until soft.
  2. Toss with the bread cubes, herbs and stock.
  3. Bake covered at 350°F for 30 minutes, then uncovered until golden.

3. Honey Glazed Carrots

Tender carrots glossed with honey and butter are the sweet, jewel-bright side that makes the whole table look festive. A sprinkle of parsley is all the garnish they need.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs carrots, peeled and sliced
  • 3 tablespoons honey
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Parsley, to garnish
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Simmer the carrots until just tender, then drain.
  2. Melt the butter and honey in the pan and toss the carrots to glaze.
  3. Finish with lemon juice, salt, pepper and parsley.

4. Roasted Brussels Sprouts with Bacon

Crispy-edged Brussels sprouts tossed with salty bacon are the side that turns sprout skeptics into believers. Roast them hot so the edges caramelize and crunch.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs Brussels sprouts, halved
  • 4 slices bacon, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Toss the sprouts with oil, garlic, salt and pepper.
  2. Scatter over the bacon and roast at 425°F for 25 minutes.
  3. Stir halfway through until crisp and golden.

5. Green Bean Casserole

Tender green beans in a creamy mushroom sauce under a golden, crunchy onion topping are the definition of a holiday classic. This is the dish that disappears first every single year.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs green beans, trimmed
  • 1 can cream of mushroom soup
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 cup crispy fried onions
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Blanch the green beans for 4 minutes and drain.
  2. Stir with the soup, milk, salt and pepper in a baking dish.
  3. Top with fried onions and bake at 350°F for 25 minutes.

6. Roasted Chestnuts

Smoky, buttery roasted chestnuts with crisped edges and tender centers are the old-fashioned Christmas side that fills the house with holiday scent. Score, roast and peel them while they are still warm.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs fresh chestnuts
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon flaky salt
  • 1 teaspoon chopped rosemary (optional)

Directions

  1. Score an X on the flat side of each chestnut.
  2. Soak in warm water for 10 minutes, then toss with butter and salt.
  3. Roast at 425°F for 20-25 minutes until the shells peel back, then peel while warm.

7. Cranberry Sauce

Bright, jewel-red cranberry sauce with a hint of orange is the tart-sweet spark that cuts through every rich dish on the table. Homemade takes fifteen minutes and beats the can every time.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz fresh cranberries
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • Zest of 1 orange
  • Pinch of cinnamon

Directions

  1. Simmer the water, sugar and orange zest until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Add the cranberries and cook until they burst, about 10 minutes.
  3. Cool to thicken, then chill before serving.

8. Baked Apples

Warm baked apples stuffed with oats, nuts and cinnamon are the cozy sweet side that doubles as a light dessert. They smell like Christmas and taste like a hug.

Ingredients

  • 6 firm apples
  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1/3 cup chopped walnuts
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup apple cider

Directions

  1. Core the apples and score a line around the middle.
  2. Fill with the oat, walnut, sugar and cinnamon mix and dot with butter.
  3. Pour cider into the dish and bake at 375°F for 40 minutes until tender.

9. Roasted Parsnips and Carrots

Sweet parsnips and carrots roasted until caramelized at the edges are the simplest, most reliable side on this list. A drizzle of honey and fresh thyme takes them over the top.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb parsnips, cut into batons
  • 1 lb carrots, cut into batons
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon thyme leaves
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Toss the vegetables with oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Roast at 425°F for 30 minutes, turning once.
  3. Drizzle with honey and thyme and roast 5 minutes more.

10. Cheesy Scalloped Potatoes

Paper-thin potatoes layered with cream, garlic and bubbling cheese bake into the golden, saucy dish everyone asks about. Let it rest before slicing so the layers hold.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/2 cups grated cheddar
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Warm the cream with garlic, salt and pepper.
  2. Layer the potatoes and cheese in a buttered dish, pouring over the cream.
  3. Bake covered at 375°F for 45 minutes, then uncovered until golden.

11. Brussels Sprouts Skewers

Charred Brussels sprouts threaded onto skewers with a garlicky glaze are the fun, finger-food side everyone grabs first. They roast evenly and look great on the table.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs Brussels sprouts, halved
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • Salt and pepper
  • Wooden skewers, soaked

Directions

  1. Toss the sprouts with oil, garlic, vinegar, honey and seasoning.
  2. Thread onto soaked skewers, cut side out.
  3. Roast at 425°F for 20 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges.

12. Maple Roasted Butternut Squash

Cubes of butternut squash roasted with maple syrup and a pinch of chili turn sweet, sticky and gorgeously golden. They add color and sunshine to any holiday plate.

Ingredients

  • 1 butternut squash, peeled and cubed
  • 2 tablespoons maple syrup
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Toss the squash with oil, maple syrup and seasoning.
  2. Spread on a baking sheet in a single layer.
  3. Roast at 425°F for 30 minutes, turning once, until caramelized.

13. Garlic Butter Green Beans

Crisp-tender green beans tossed in garlic butter with a squeeze of lemon are the fresh, bright counterpoint to all the rich dishes. They take ten minutes and taste like you tried much harder.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs green beans, trimmed
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Blanch the beans for 3 minutes, then drain.
  2. Melt the butter and cook the garlic until golden.
  3. Toss in the beans and lemon juice and serve at once.

14. Mince Pies

Buttery little mince pies with spiced fruit filling and snowflake pastry tops are the festive bite that ends the Christmas meal on a sweet note. Make a double batch — they vanish fast.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mincemeat
  • 1 batch shortcrust pastry (or 2 sheets)
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • Powdered sugar, for dusting
  • Zest of 1 orange

Directions

  1. Stir the orange zest into the mincemeat.
  2. Line a muffin tin with pastry rounds, fill with mincemeat and top with pastry stars or snowflakes.
  3. Brush with egg and bake at 400°F for 15-18 minutes until golden, then dust with sugar.

15. Roasted Cauliflower with Parmesan

Cauliflower roasted until nutty and golden, then showered with Parmesan, is the quiet overachiever of the table. Even cauliflower skeptics go back for seconds.

Ingredients

  • 1 large cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Toss the florets with oil, garlic, salt and pepper.
  2. Roast at 425°F for 25 minutes until golden.
  3. Shower with Parmesan and roast 5 minutes more.

16. Brown Butter Sage Sweet Potatoes

Roasted sweet potatoes drizzled with nutty brown butter and crispy sage leaves are the elegant, grown-up way to serve sweet potatoes. No marshmallows needed.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 12 sage leaves
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Toss the sweet potatoes with oil and roast at 425°F for 30 minutes.
  2. Cook the butter until brown and nutty, then crisp the sage in it.
  3. Drizzle over the potatoes and serve warm.

17. Creamy Macaroni and Cheese

Elbow pasta in a velvety three-cheese sauce with a golden baked top is the comfort dish every generation agrees on. Make a double batch — it vanishes fast.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb elbow macaroni
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 3 cups milk
  • 2 cups cheddar, grated
  • 1 cup Gruyère, grated
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Cook the pasta until just shy of done and drain.
  2. Make a roux with butter and flour, whisk in the milk, then melt in the cheeses.
  3. Combine, top with breadcrumbs and bake at 375°F for 25 minutes.

18. Brussels Sprouts with Balsamic Glaze

Caramelized Brussels sprouts finished with a sticky balsamic glaze are sweet, tangy and dangerously snackable. The glaze turns an everyday vegetable into a holiday dish.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs Brussels sprouts, halved
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/4 cup balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Roast the sprouts with oil at 425°F for 25 minutes.
  2. Simmer the balsamic and honey into a glaze.
  3. Toss the sprouts in the glaze and serve warm.

19. Roasted Beet Salad with Goat Cheese

Earthy roasted beets, creamy goat cheese and crunchy walnuts over soft leaves make the jewel-toned salad that brightens a heavy table. It looks as good as it tastes.

Ingredients

  • 4 beets, roasted and sliced
  • 4 cups mixed leaves
  • 4 oz goat cheese, crumbled
  • 1/2 cup walnuts, toasted
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Roast the beets at 400°F until tender, then cool and slice.
  2. Whisk the oil, vinegar, salt and pepper into a dressing.
  3. Arrange the leaves, beets, goat cheese and walnuts and drizzle with dressing.

20. Classic Dinner Rolls

Soft, buttery dinner rolls with golden tops are the humble hero every Christmas table needs. They are made for mopping up gravy and cranberry sauce.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups flour
  • 1 tablespoon yeast
  • 1 1/4 cups warm milk
  • 3 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 egg, beaten

Directions

  1. Mix and knead the dough until smooth, then rise for 1 hour.
  2. Shape into rolls and rise for 30 minutes more.
  3. Brush with egg and bake at 375°F for 18 minutes.

21. Wild Rice Pilaf with Cranberries

Nutty wild rice studded with tart cranberries, toasted pecans and fresh herbs is the festive, gluten-free side that feels a little bit fancy. It holds beautifully on a buffet.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups wild rice blend
  • 3 cups vegetable stock
  • 1/2 cup dried cranberries
  • 1/2 cup pecans, toasted
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • Parsley, to garnish
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Sauté the onion in butter, then add the rice and stock.
  2. Simmer covered for 45 minutes until tender.
  3. Fold in the cranberries, pecans and parsley.

22. Glazed Pearl Onions

Tiny pearl onions glazed in butter, sugar and balsamic until glossy and sweet are the old-fashioned side that deserves a comeback. They shine beside any roast.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pearl onions, peeled
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
  • 1 cup vegetable stock
  • Thyme leaves
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Blanch and peel the onions.
  2. Simmer with butter, sugar, vinegar and stock for 20 minutes.
  3. Reduce the liquid to a glaze and scatter with thyme.

23. Cheesy Broccoli Casserole

Broccoli folded through a creamy cheese sauce with a buttery crumb topping is the casserole that makes kids ask for more broccoli. It is pure comfort in a dish.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs broccoli florets
  • 1 can cream of mushroom soup
  • 1 1/2 cups cheddar, grated
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Blanch the broccoli for 3 minutes and drain.
  2. Stir with the soup, cheese and mayonnaise in a baking dish.
  3. Top with buttered breadcrumbs and bake at 350°F for 25 minutes.

24. Roasted Root Vegetables

Carrots, parsnips, turnips and sweet potatoes roasted together with rosemary turn into a caramelized, earthy tumble that fills the house with holiday smells.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs mixed root vegetables, cubed
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 2 garlic cloves, smashed
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Toss the vegetables with oil, rosemary and garlic.
  2. Spread on baking sheets without crowding.
  3. Roast at 425°F for 35 minutes, turning once, until golden.

25. Creamy Corn Pudding

Sweet corn baked into a soft, custardy pudding with a golden top is the Southern holiday side that tastes like sunshine. It sits happily between the casseroles and the salads.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups corn kernels
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 3 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Blend half the corn with the eggs, milk and flour.
  2. Stir in the remaining corn, butter and sugar.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 45 minutes until set and golden.

26. Sautéed Mushrooms with Thyme

Golden mushrooms sautéed with garlic, butter and fresh thyme are the earthy, savory side that goes with everything on the table. Do not crowd the pan — that is the whole secret.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoon thyme leaves
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Sear the mushrooms in batches in hot butter until golden.
  2. Add the garlic and thyme and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Splash in the soy sauce and serve warm.

27. Pomegranate Winter Salad

Crisp winter leaves, ruby pomegranate seeds, feta and candied pecans with a citrus dressing make the salad that looks like Christmas itself. It is the lightest dish on a heavy table.

Ingredients

  • 6 cups winter salad leaves
  • 1 cup pomegranate seeds
  • 4 oz feta, crumbled
  • 1/2 cup candied pecans
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon orange juice
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Whisk the oil, orange juice, honey, salt and pepper.
  2. Toss the leaves with half the dressing.
  3. Top with pomegranate, feta and pecans and drizzle with the rest.

28. Garlic Roasted Potatoes

Crispy golden potatoes with crackly skins and soft centers, tossed with garlic and rosemary, are the side no one ever leaves behind. Parboil first for the crispiest edges.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs potatoes, cubed
  • 4 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 1 sprig rosemary
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Parboil the potatoes for 8 minutes, then drain and rough up the edges.
  2. Toss with oil, garlic and rosemary.
  3. Roast at 425°F for 35 minutes until deeply golden.

29. Cauliflower Cheese

Tender cauliflower under a rich, golden cheese sauce with a crisp breadcrumb top is the British Christmas classic that belongs on every table. It is comfort in its purest form.

Ingredients

  • 1 large cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 3 tablespoons flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1 1/2 cups cheddar, grated
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • Pinch of mustard powder
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Steam the cauliflower until just tender.
  2. Make a roux with butter and flour, whisk in the milk, then melt in the cheese and mustard.
  3. Pour over the cauliflower, top with breadcrumbs and bake until golden.

30. Honey Mustard Roasted Parsnips

Parsnips roasted with a honey mustard glaze turn sweet, tangy and beautifully caramelized. They are the grown-up twist on a classic roast vegetable.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs parsnips, cut into batons
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1 tablespoon wholegrain mustard
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Thyme leaves
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Toss the parsnips with oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Roast at 425°F for 25 minutes.
  3. Brush with the honey mustard and roast 10 minutes more until glazed.

31. Deviled Eggs

Creamy deviled eggs dusted with paprika and chives are the retro holiday side that disappears from the platter first. They are make-ahead friendly and endlessly popular.

Ingredients

  • 12 eggs
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon white vinegar
  • Paprika, for dusting
  • 2 tablespoons chopped chives
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Hard-boil the eggs, cool, peel and halve lengthwise.
  2. Mash the yolks with mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar and seasoning until smooth.
  3. Pipe or spoon back into the whites and finish with paprika and chives.

32. Peas with Mint and Butter

Sweet peas tossed with butter and fresh mint are the simplest, brightest green side on the table. They take five minutes and taste like spring in December.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups peas
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons mint, chopped
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the peas and cook for 3 minutes until just tender.
  3. Toss with mint, salt and pepper and serve.

33. Roasted Sweet Potatoes with Pecans

Caramelized sweet potato rounds topped with buttery toasted pecans are the sweet-savory side with real crunch. A drizzle of maple at the end makes them irresistible.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs sweet potatoes, sliced into rounds
  • 1 cup pecans, chopped
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons maple syrup
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Toss the sweet potato rounds with oil, cinnamon, salt and pepper.
  2. Roast at 425°F for 25 minutes, flipping once.
  3. Top with pecans and maple syrup and roast 5 minutes more.

34. Creamy Leek Gratin

Silky leeks baked in cream with Gruyère and a golden crumb top are the elegant French side that feels special without any fuss. They melt into pure luxury.

Ingredients

  • 4 large leeks, sliced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup Gruyère, grated
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Sauté the leeks in butter until soft.
  2. Stir in the cream and nutmeg and simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Top with Gruyère and breadcrumbs and bake until golden.

35. Festive Roasted Vegetables with Herbs

A big tray of carrots, parsnips, Brussels sprouts and red onion roasted with rosemary and thyme is the easiest way to feed a crowd. One tray, endless color, zero fuss.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs mixed vegetables, chopped
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 1 tablespoon thyme leaves
  • 2 garlic cloves, smashed
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Toss everything with oil and seasoning.
  2. Spread on baking sheets without crowding.
  3. Roast at 425°F for 35 minutes, turning once, until caramelized.

Christmas Side Dishes Tips

  • Make casseroles, cranberry sauce and stuffing prep the day before so Christmas day stays relaxed.
  • Roast vegetables first at high heat, slide casseroles in after and bake the rolls last so everything lands hot at once.
  • Keep mashed potatoes warm in a slow cooker or a covered pot over low heat with a knob of butter on top.
  • Set the table the night before and finish each dish with fresh herbs or pomegranate seeds for instant Christmas charm.

Final Thoughts

With these 35 Christmas side dishes, your holiday table will be full of warm, festive and crowd-pleasing dishes for every guest and every craving. Pick a few favorites, cook them with love, and enjoy the merriest dinner of the season together.
