35+ Christmas Punch Recipes to Keep the Party Pouring

Oct 30, 2026

Nothing gets a Christmas party pouring like a big beautiful punch bowl, and these Christmas punch recipes cover every style your holiday table needs. From classic cranberry and sparkling apple cider to creamy eggnog punch, festive sangria and bright pomegranate sparklers, this list has a bowl for every guest — including the kids.

Work through these 35 Christmas punch recipes one gathering at a time, or pick a few favorites for your Christmas party. Every recipe below comes with simple ingredients and easy directions, so you can mix, chill, pour and enjoy the merriest toast of the season.

1. Classic Cranberry Christmas Punch

Tart cranberry juice, sweet pineapple and fizzy ginger ale make the Christmas classic every party table needs. Float fresh cranberries and orange slices on top for a bowl that looks as good as it tastes.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups cranberry juice
  • 2 cups pineapple juice
  • 2 cups ginger ale
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 1 cup fresh cranberries
  • Ice

Directions

  1. Stir together the cranberry and pineapple juice in a large punch bowl.
  2. Add the orange slices, cranberries and plenty of ice.
  3. Pour in the ginger ale just before serving and ladle into glasses.

2. Pomegranate Prosecco Punch

Ruby pomegranate juice and bubbly prosecco with a rosemary sprig turn an ordinary evening into a celebration. It is elegant, jewel-toned and dangerously easy to refill.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups pomegranate juice
  • 1 bottle prosecco, chilled
  • 1 cup pomegranate seeds
  • 4 rosemary sprigs
  • 1/2 cup orange liqueur (optional)
  • Ice

Directions

  1. Pour the pomegranate juice and liqueur into a punch bowl with ice.
  2. Scatter in the pomegranate seeds and rosemary sprigs.
  3. Top with chilled prosecco right before serving.

3. Sparkling Apple Cider Punch

Crisp apple cider, sparkling water and warm cinnamon make a golden punch that tastes like Christmas in a glass. Apple slices and cinnamon sticks do all the decorating for you.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups apple cider
  • 2 cups sparkling water
  • 2 apples, sliced
  • 3 cinnamon sticks
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • Ice

Directions

  1. Stir the cider and lemon juice together in a punch bowl.
  2. Add the apple slices, cinnamon sticks and ice.
  3. Pour in the sparkling water last and serve immediately.

5. Creamy Eggnog Punch

Rich eggnog, vanilla and a dusting of cinnamon make a creamy punch that doubles as dessert. Serve it chilled with whipped cream for the coziest sip of the season.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups eggnog, chilled
  • 1 cup vanilla ice cream, softened
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/2 teaspoon nutmeg
  • Whipped cream
  • Cinnamon, for dusting

Directions

  1. Whisk the eggnog, ice cream and vanilla until smooth.
  2. Pour into a punch bowl and dust generously with nutmeg.
  3. Ladle into cups and top with whipped cream and cinnamon.

6. Warm Spiced Wassail Punch

Simmered cider, citrus and mulling spices fill the whole house with Christmas as this wassail warms. Ladle it hot into mugs for carol singers and cold-night guests alike.

Ingredients

  • 6 cups apple cider
  • 2 cups cranberry juice
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 3 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 2 star anise
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar

Directions

  1. Combine everything in a large pot and bring to a gentle simmer.
  2. Simmer on low for 30 minutes so the spices bloom.
  3. Ladle hot into mugs, straining out the whole spices if you like.

7. Raspberry Sherbet Punch

Pink raspberry sherbet melting into lemon-lime soda makes a frothy, pastel punch kids go wild for. It is sweet, fizzy and impossibly pretty in a glass bowl.

Ingredients

  • 1 quart raspberry sherbet
  • 2 cups pineapple juice
  • 2 cups lemon-lime soda
  • Fresh raspberries
  • Ice (optional)

Directions

  1. Scoop the sherbet into a punch bowl and pour over the pineapple juice.
  2. Stir gently until slightly melted and frothy.
  3. Top with lemon-lime soda and raspberries just before serving.

8. Pineapple Coconut Punch

Creamy coconut, sweet pineapple and a cherry on top bring a little tropical sunshine to a winter party. Serve it in coconut shells if you want the whole table talking.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups pineapple juice
  • 2 cups coconut cream
  • 1 cup coconut water
  • Pineapple wedges
  • Maraschino cherries
  • Ice

Directions

  1. Whisk the pineapple juice, coconut cream and coconut water until smooth.
  2. Pour over ice in a punch bowl or coconut shells.
  3. Garnish with pineapple wedges and cherries.

9. Gingerbread Spice Punch

Ginger, molasses and cinnamon turn a simple punch into liquid gingerbread. It tastes like the cookie tray and the drinks table finally met.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups apple cider
  • 1 cup ginger beer
  • 2 tablespoons molasses
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • Orange slices
  • Gingerbread cookies, for serving

Directions

  1. Warm the cider with molasses, ginger and cinnamon until the molasses dissolves.
  2. Cool completely, then pour into a punch bowl with orange slices.
  3. Top with ginger beer and serve with gingerbread cookies.

10. Candy Cane Punch

Cool peppermint, creamy vanilla and a candy cane stirrer make a punch that tastes like Christmas morning. The red-and-white garnish does the decorating all by itself.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups milk
  • 1 cup vanilla ice cream, softened
  • 1/2 teaspoon peppermint extract
  • Crushed candy canes
  • Candy canes, for stirring
  • Whipped cream

Directions

  1. Blend the milk, ice cream and peppermint extract until smooth.
  2. Pour into a punch bowl and rim glasses with crushed candy cane if you like.
  3. Serve with whipped cream and a candy cane stirrer in each glass.

14. Holiday Red Sangria Punch

Red wine, brandy and a rainbow of sliced fruit make a sangria punch that only gets better as it chills. Make it the night before and let the fruit do the work.

Ingredients

  • 1 bottle red wine
  • 1 cup brandy
  • 2 cups cranberry juice
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 1 apple, sliced
  • 1 cup cranberries
  • 2 cups lemon-lime soda

Directions

  1. Combine the wine, brandy, juice and fruit in a large bowl or pitcher.
  2. Chill for at least 4 hours, or overnight for the best flavor.
  3. Top with lemon-lime soda and ice just before serving.

15. Spiced Plum Punch

19. Hot Buttered Rum Punch

Buttery brown sugar, rum and warm spices make a mug of pure Christmas comfort. Serve it hot with an orange slice for the coziest toast of the night.

Ingredients

  • 6 cups hot water
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar

Directions

  2. Stir in the rum and ladle into warm mugs.
  3. Garnish with an orange slice and serve hot.

20. Slow Cooker Christmas Punch

Everything simmers together in the slow cooker for a punch that stays warm all party long. Set it on low, ladle as guests arrive, and never tend a pot again.

Ingredients

  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 3 cinnamon sticks
  • 6 cloves
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar

Directions

  1. Combine everything in a slow cooker.
  2. Cook on low for 2 hours until fragrant.
  3. Ladle warm into mugs, keeping the cooker on warm for refills.

21. Frozen Cranberry Slush Punch

Frozen cranberry slush scooped into ginger ale makes a frosty, ruby punch that stays cold for hours. It is the make-ahead miracle for big Christmas crowds.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups cranberry juice
  • 2 cups pineapple juice
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • 2 cups ginger ale, chilled
  • Mint and lime, for garnish

Directions

  1. Freeze the cranberry, pineapple and lemon juice overnight in a container.
  2. Scrape into slush and scoop into a punch bowl.
  3. Pour over the ginger ale and garnish with mint and lime.

Christmas Punch Tips

  • Chill every ingredient before mixing so the punch stays cold without watering down too fast.
  • Freeze juice or punch in a bundt pan for a festive ice ring that keeps the bowl cold and pretty.
  • Add sparkling water, soda or prosecco at the very last minute so the fizz survives until the first toast.
  • Set out a ladle, small cups and a few garnish bowls so guests can serve themselves all night.
