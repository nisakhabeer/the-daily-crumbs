Easy Garlic Butter Pasta — Ready in 20 Minutes

Oct 20, 2026

This easy garlic butter pasta is my go-to weeknight favorite — a simple, cozy bowl of spaghetti tossed in golden garlic butter with a little heat from chili flakes. It uses pantry staples and is on the table in just 20 minutes.

Prep time: 5 mins  |  Cook time: 15 mins  |  Serves: 2–3

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
  • Salt, to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Grated parmesan, to serve
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1/4 cup reserved pasta water

Method

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti until al dente. Before draining, scoop out 1/4 cup of the pasta water and set it aside.
  2. While the pasta cooks, melt the butter in a large pan over medium heat. Add the sliced garlic and cook gently, stirring often, until golden and fragrant — don’t let it brown too much.
  3. Stir in the chili flakes, then pour in the reserved pasta water. Let it bubble for a minute to form a light, silky sauce.
  4. Add the drained spaghetti to the pan and toss everything together so the pasta is well coated.
  5. Season with salt and black pepper, then sprinkle over the grated parmesan and fresh parsley.
  6. Serve hot, straight from the pan.

Tip

For the best flavor, use real butter and fresh garlic — and save a little extra parmesan for the table. A squeeze of lemon juice at the end brightens everything up too!
