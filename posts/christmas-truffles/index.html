35+ Christmas Truffles That Melt in Your Mouth

Oct 26, 2026

When the fairy lights go up and the house smells like chocolate, only one bite-sized treat belongs on every table: Christmas truffles. From dark chocolate classics and salted caramel centers to coconut snowballs and nutty pralines, these Christmas truffles cover every craving the season brings.

Work through this list of 35 truffles one batch at a time, or pick your favorites for a gift box or dessert table that guests will talk about long after the holidays. Every truffle below comes with simple ingredients and easy directions, so even a first-time candy maker can fill the house with something wonderful.

1. Classic Dark Chocolate Truffles

Deep, dark and silky, these classic truffles are the ones everyone reaches for first. A glossy drizzle on top makes them look straight from a chocolate shop.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 2 oz dark chocolate, melted, to drizzle

Directions

  1. Heat the cream until steaming and pour over the chopped chocolate and butter.
  2. Stir until smooth, then chill 2 hours until firm enough to scoop.
  3. Roll into small balls, drizzle with melted chocolate and chill until set.

2. Milk Chocolate Truffles

Sweet, creamy milk chocolate truffles with a soft ganache center are pure comfort. They are the easiest truffles to love and the first to disappear.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz milk chocolate, chopped
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Cocoa powder, for rolling

Directions

  1. Heat the cream until steaming and pour over the chocolate and butter.
  2. Stir until smooth and chill 2 hours until scoopable.
  3. Roll into balls, coat lightly in cocoa and chill until firm.

3. White Chocolate Truffles

Creamy white chocolate truffles with a vanilla-sweet center look as pretty as they taste. A scatter of sprinkles turns them into instant party food.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz white chocolate, chopped
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • Sprinkles, to coat

Directions

  1. Heat the cream and pour over the white chocolate, butter and vanilla.
  2. Stir until smooth and chill 2 hours until firm.
  3. Roll into balls, coat in sprinkles and chill until set.

4. Salted Caramel Truffles

A molten salted caramel heart inside dark chocolate is the surprise everyone hopes for. Sweet, salty and completely irresistible.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup thick caramel sauce
  • 1 teaspoon flaky sea salt
  • Cocoa powder, for rolling

Directions

  1. Make the ganache with cream and chocolate and chill until scoopable.
  2. Flatten each portion, tuck in a small spoonful of caramel with a pinch of salt, and seal into a ball.
  3. Roll in cocoa and chill until firm.

5. Coconut Snowball Truffles

Rolled in snowy shredded coconut, these truffles look like little snowballs on the dessert table. Their creamy centers taste like a tropical holiday.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz white chocolate, chopped
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded coconut, divided

Directions

  1. Melt the white chocolate with the cream and condensed milk until smooth.
  2. Stir in 1/2 cup of the coconut and chill 2 hours.
  3. Roll into balls and coat thickly in the remaining coconut.

6. Cocoa-Dusted Truffles

The original French-style truffle, rolled in bitter cocoa for a grown-up chocolate hit. Simple, elegant and deeply chocolatey.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder

Directions

  1. Heat the cream and pour over the chocolate and butter, stirring until smooth.
  2. Chill 2 hours, then scoop and roll into rough balls.
  3. Toss generously in cocoa powder and chill until serving.

7. Hazelnut Praline Truffles

Crunchy hazelnut praline folded into chocolate ganache gives these truffles a candy-bar heart. They taste like the finest filled chocolates.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz milk chocolate, chopped
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup hazelnut praline paste
  • 1/2 cup chopped toasted hazelnuts

Directions

  1. Heat the cream and pour over the chocolate, stirring until smooth.
  2. Blend in the praline paste and chill 2 hours.
  3. Roll into balls and coat in chopped hazelnuts.

8. Almond Crunch Truffles

Toasted almonds bring crunch to a soft chocolate center in these rustic truffles. They are hearty, nutty and wonderful with coffee.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup chopped toasted almonds
  • 1 teaspoon almond extract

Directions

  1. Make the ganache with cream, chocolate and almond extract and chill until firm.
  2. Roll into balls and press into the chopped almonds.
  3. Chill until set and serve at cool room temperature.

9. Pistachio Truffles

Salty green pistachios over dark chocolate make these truffles as beautiful as they are delicious. They are the jewel of any holiday tin.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 3/4 cup chopped salted pistachios
  • Cocoa powder, for dusting

Directions

  1. Make the ganache and chill 2 hours until scoopable.
  2. Roll into balls and coat in chopped pistachios.
  3. Dust lightly with cocoa and chill until firm.

10. Holiday Nut Truffles

Dressed with nuts and holiday sparkle, these truffles are made for Christmas gifting. Box them up with ribbon for the easiest homemade present.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz mixed chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup mixed chopped nuts
  • Holiday sprinkles, to decorate

Directions

  1. Make the ganache and chill until firm enough to scoop.
  2. Roll into balls and coat in mixed nuts.
  3. Top with a few holiday sprinkles and chill until set.

11. Evergreen White Chocolate Truffles

White chocolate truffles with a whisper of mint, served beside evergreen, taste like a walk through a snowy forest. Cool, creamy and festive.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz white chocolate, chopped
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 teaspoon peppermint extract
  • 1 cup shredded coconut, for rolling

Directions

  1. Heat the cream and pour over the white chocolate with the peppermint extract.
  2. Stir until smooth and chill 2 hours.
  3. Roll into balls, coat in coconut and chill until firm.

12. Cozy Winter Cocoa Truffles

Made to be nibbled beside a mug of hot cocoa, these truffles are winter comfort in bite size. Serve them warm from the kitchen on cold nights.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • Mini marshmallows, to serve

Directions

  1. Make a spiced ganache with cream, chocolate, cocoa and cinnamon and chill until firm.
  2. Roll into balls and dust with extra cocoa.
  3. Serve beside hot cocoa with mini marshmallows.

13. Glass Dish Truffles

Piled in a glass dish, these glossy homemade truffles show off every swirl and coating. They are the centerpiece of a simple dessert spread.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Assorted coatings: cocoa, nuts, coconut

Directions

  1. Make the ganache and chill 2 hours until scoopable.
  2. Roll into balls and divide among the coatings.
  3. Pile into a glass dish and chill until serving.

14. Mocha Espresso Truffles

A shot of espresso deepens the chocolate in these truffles and makes them taste like your favorite café. Coffee lovers will take two.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon instant espresso
  • Cocoa powder mixed with espresso powder, for rolling

Directions

  1. Dissolve the espresso in the hot cream, then pour over the chocolate.
  2. Stir until smooth and chill 2 hours.
  3. Roll into balls and coat in the cocoa-espresso mix.

15. Oat Crunch Truffles

Toasted oats give these chocolate balls a wholesome crunch and a rustic look. They are a sturdier truffle that travels well in gift boxes.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz milk chocolate, chopped
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream
  • 3/4 cup toasted rolled oats
  • 1 tablespoon honey

Directions

  1. Melt the chocolate with the cream and honey until smooth.
  2. Stir in half the oats and chill 2 hours.
  3. Roll into balls and coat in the remaining oats.

16. Sesame Truffles

Toasted sesame seeds add a nutty crunch and a beautiful speckled coat to these truffles. They are a little different and everyone asks about them.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup toasted sesame seeds
  • 1 teaspoon honey

Directions

  1. Make the ganache with cream, chocolate and honey and chill until firm.
  2. Roll into balls and coat thickly in sesame seeds.
  3. Set in mini cupcake liners and chill until firm.

17. Chocolate Sprinkle Brigadeiros

Brazil's beloved brigadeiros — fudgy chocolate balls rolled in sprinkles — are the easiest truffles of all. One pan, one tray, endless smiles.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 3 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Chocolate sprinkles, to coat

Directions

  1. Cook the condensed milk, cocoa and butter on low, stirring, until thick enough to hold its shape, about 10 minutes.
  2. Cool completely, butter your hands and roll into small balls.
  3. Coat in sprinkles and set in mini paper cups.

18. Brazilian Brigadeiro Box

A whole box of glossy brigadeiros is the gift that looks like you spent hours. Make a big batch — they keep beautifully for days.

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (14 oz each) sweetened condensed milk
  • 6 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • Chocolate sprinkles and cocoa, to coat

Directions

  1. Cook the condensed milk, cocoa and butter until thick and glossy, about 12 minutes.
  2. Cool, then roll into balls with buttered hands.
  3. Coat half in sprinkles and half in cocoa, then box up.

19. Assorted Brazilian Truffles

A tray of Brazilian truffles with every topping — sprinkles, nuts, coconut and cocoa — feels like a candy shop at home. Let guests pick their favorite.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 3 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Sprinkles, nuts, coconut and cocoa, to coat

Directions

  1. Cook the brigadeiro mixture until thick, then cool completely.
  2. Roll into balls and divide among the coatings.
  3. Arrange on a tray and chill until serving.

20. Coconut Brigadeiros

Snowy coconut brigadeiros, sweet and chewy, are the prettiest white truffles on the tray. They pair perfectly with dark chocolate ones.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 cup shredded coconut, plus extra for rolling
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla

Directions

  1. Cook the condensed milk, butter and coconut on low until thick, about 10 minutes.
  2. Cool, then roll into balls with buttered hands.
  3. Coat in extra coconut and chill until firm.

21. White Chocolate Coconut Truffles

White chocolate and coconut melt into the creamiest truffle centers, with a snowy coat on the outside. They taste like a holiday beach escape.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz white chocolate, chopped
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded coconut, divided

Directions

  1. Melt the white chocolate with the cream and condensed milk until smooth.
  2. Stir in 1/2 cup coconut and chill 2 hours.
  3. Roll into balls and coat in the remaining coconut.

22. Chocolate Coconut Confections

Dark chocolate shells around sweet coconut centers are a classic pairing for a reason. Boxed together, they look like a professional assortment.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup sweetened shredded coconut
  • 1/4 cup sweetened condensed milk

Directions

  1. Stir the coconut and condensed milk into a thick filling and chill until firm.
  2. Shape into small centers and freeze 30 minutes.
  3. Dip in melted chocolate ganache and chill until set.

23. Pink Strawberry Truffles

Pretty in pink, these strawberry truffles get their color and flavor from real freeze-dried berries. They are sweet, tangy and gorgeous.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz white chocolate, chopped
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup freeze-dried strawberries, crushed
  • Pink sprinkles, to coat

Directions

  1. Melt the white chocolate with the cream until smooth.
  2. Stir in half the crushed strawberries and chill 2 hours.
  3. Roll into balls and coat in the remaining berries and sprinkles.

24. Mixed Nut Truffles

Every bite of these truffles has a different nut — almond, hazelnut, pecan — for a crunchy surprise. They are the nut lover's truffle.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup mixed chopped nuts
  • 1 tablespoon butter

Directions

  1. Make the ganache and chill until scoopable.
  2. Roll into balls and press into the mixed nuts.
  3. Chill until firm and serve at cool room temperature.

25. Assorted Gourmet Truffles

One ganache, endless coatings — this is how you fill a whole dessert table from a single batch. Cocoa, nuts, coconut and sprinkles cover them all.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz mixed chocolate, chopped
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Cocoa, nuts, coconut and sprinkles, for coating

Directions

  1. Make the ganache and chill until firm enough to scoop.
  2. Roll into balls and divide into four bowls.
  3. Coat each batch in a different coating and chill until set.

26. Elegant Gift Box Truffles

Truffles nestled in a gift box with tissue and ribbon are the most personal present you can give. Make two boxes — one to give, one to keep.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz mixed chocolate, chopped
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream
  • Assorted coatings
  • Mini paper cups and a gift box

Directions

  1. Make the ganache and chill, then roll and coat in assorted coatings.
  2. Set each truffle in a mini paper cup.
  3. Nestle into a gift box with tissue and tie with ribbon.

27. Truffles in a Crystal Bowl

Piled high in a crystal bowl, glossy truffles catch the light and everyone's eye. This is the easiest elegant dessert for a party.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz mixed chocolate, chopped
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Cocoa and powdered sugar, for dusting

Directions

  1. Make the ganache and chill until scoopable.
  2. Roll into balls and dust half in cocoa and half in powdered sugar.
  3. Pile into a crystal bowl and chill until serving.

28. Rustic Chocolate Balls

Roughly rolled and generously cocoa-dusted, these rustic balls are comfort food at its best. No perfection needed — their charm is homemade.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder

Directions

  1. Make the ganache and chill 2 hours until firm.
  2. Scoop and roll into rough, rustic balls.
  3. Toss in cocoa and pile into a ceramic bowl.

29. Vintage Tin Truffles

Truffles tucked into a vintage tin feel like a gift from another era. They keep well, travel well and look wonderful on the table.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Chopped nuts and cocoa, for coating

Directions

  1. Make the ganache and chill until scoopable.
  2. Roll into balls and coat half in nuts and half in cocoa.
  3. Layer into a tin with parchment and chill until gifting.

30. Artisan Dish Truffles

Served in a decorative dish, these artisan truffles turn an ordinary evening into an occasion. A few beautiful truffles beat a whole cake.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Edible gold dust or cocoa, to finish

Directions

  1. Make the ganache and chill 2 hours until firm.
  2. Roll into smooth, even balls.
  3. Finish with a touch of gold dust or cocoa and arrange in a dish.

31. Elegant Packaging Truffles

Wrapped like a boutique box, these truffles prove that presentation is half the treat. They are made for weddings, parties and special gifts.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz mixed chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • Assorted coatings
  • Small boxes and ribbon

Directions

  1. Make the ganache, chill, roll and coat in assorted coatings.
  2. Place in mini cups and pack into small boxes.
  3. Tie with ribbon and chill until gifting.

32. Floral Plate Truffles

Assorted truffles arranged on a floral plate look like they came from a Parisian pâtisserie. Pretty plates make simple truffles feel special.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz mixed chocolate, chopped
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream
  • Assorted coatings
  • A pretty plate, to serve

Directions

  1. Make the ganache and chill, then roll and coat in assorted coatings.
  2. Arrange in a ring on a floral plate.
  3. Chill until serving and bring to the table on the plate.

33. Praline Truffles on White Plate

Seen from above, a white plate of praline truffles is a study in chocolate — dark, milk and nutty, all in a row. Simple plating, stunning result.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz milk chocolate, chopped
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup hazelnut praline paste
  • Cocoa powder, for dusting

Directions

  1. Blend the praline paste into warm ganache and chill until firm.
  2. Roll into even balls and dust with cocoa.
  3. Arrange on a white plate and chill until serving.

34. Praline Truffles on Black Plate

Against a black plate, glossy praline truffles look dramatic and luxurious. This is the plating trick for a dinner-party dessert.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup hazelnut praline paste
  • Chopped hazelnuts, to coat

Directions

  1. Blend the praline paste into warm ganache and chill until firm.
  2. Roll into balls and coat in chopped hazelnuts.
  3. Arrange on a black plate and chill until serving.

35. Classic Praline Chocolates

The timeless praline — nutty, chocolatey and perfectly bite-sized — never goes out of style. Master this one and you can make any truffle.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz milk chocolate, chopped
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream
  • 3/4 cup finely ground hazelnuts
  • 1 tablespoon butter

Directions

  1. Make the ganache and blend in the ground hazelnuts.
  2. Chill 2 hours until firm enough to scoop.
  3. Roll into balls, chill until set and serve.

Christmas Truffle Tips

  • Chill ganache until firm but scoopable — too soft and the balls will not hold their shape, too hard and they crack.
  • Butter your hands lightly before rolling so the chocolate does not stick to your fingers.
  • Make truffles ahead and freeze them uncoated, then roll in coatings on the day for the freshest finish.
  • Keep a mix of dark, milk, white and nutty truffles so every guest finds a favorite in the box.

Final Thoughts

With these 35 Christmas truffles, your holiday table will be full of dark, milk and white chocolate bites for every guest and every craving. Pick a few favorites, roll them with love, and enjoy the sweetest part of the season together.
