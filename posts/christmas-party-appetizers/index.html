25+ Christmas Party Appetizers Guests Will Love

Oct 24, 2026

A great Christmas party runs on great appetizers — the plates guests graze between conversations, top up between toasts, and remember long after the tree lights go out. These Christmas party appetizers are the ones guests circle back for all night: crisp bacon-wrapped dates, creamy whipped feta, golden spanakopita, and plenty of other hot bites, creamy dips, and fresh no-cook nibbles that look festive without keeping you in the kitchen.

Most of this list can be prepped ahead — dips whipped, skewers threaded, and pastry filled and chilled — so the oven does the last-minute work while you enjoy your own party. Mix a couple of hot Christmas party appetizers with a dip and something fresh, set everything out with small plates and napkins, and let guests help themselves. From teriyaki skewers to stuffed peppers, there is something here for every appetite at the table.

1. Bacon-Wrapped Dates with Goat Cheese

Sweet, salty, and crisp in one bite — plump dates filled with tangy goat cheese, wrapped in bacon, and baked until the edges caramelize. Finish with a warm honey drizzle and watch the plate empty fast.

Ingredients

  • 24 Medjool dates
  • 4 oz goat cheese, softened
  • 12 slices bacon, halved crosswise
  • 24 toothpicks
  • 2 tablespoons honey, warmed, for drizzling

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Cut a small slit in each date, remove the pit, and fill with about a teaspoon of goat cheese.
  3. Wrap each date with a half slice of bacon, secure with a toothpick, and place seam-side down on the sheet.
  4. Bake 15 to 18 minutes, turning once, until the bacon is crisp.
  5. Drizzle with the warm honey and serve warm.

2. Whipped Feta with Hot Honey

Cloud-soft whipped feta under a glossy swirl of chili-warmed honey — salty, sweet, and just a little spicy. Set it out with crostini and let guests scoop, spread, and go back for more.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz feta cheese, crumbled
  • 1/2 cup Greek yogurt or cream cheese, softened
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, plus more for drizzling
  • 1/3 cup honey
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • Pita chips or crostini, for serving

Directions

  1. Blend the feta, Greek yogurt, and olive oil until completely smooth and fluffy, scraping down the bowl once or twice.
  2. Spoon into a shallow bowl and swirl the top with the back of a spoon.
  3. Warm the honey with the chili flakes in a small pan until loose and fragrant, then rest 2 minutes.
  4. Pour the hot honey over the feta, scatter with thyme, and finish with a thread of olive oil.
  5. Serve at once with pita chips or crostini.

3. Spanakopita Triangles

Flaky, golden triangles with a savory spinach and feta filling — crisp outside, soft and herby inside. They freeze beautifully, so make a double batch and bake the extras straight from frozen.

Ingredients

  • 1 package phyllo pastry, thawed
  • 10 oz spinach, fresh or frozen
  • 1 cup (6 oz) crumbled feta cheese
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 4 tablespoons butter, melted
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Sauté the onion until soft, add the spinach until wilted, then squeeze the mixture very dry and let it cool.
  2. Stir in the feta, beaten egg, salt, and pepper.
  3. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Lay out a sheet of phyllo, brush with melted butter, and cut it into long strips.
  4. Place a spoonful of filling at the end of each strip and fold up into triangles, flag-style, sealing the edge with butter.
  5. Bake 18 to 20 minutes until deep golden and crisp; serve warm.

4. Cranberry Brie Crostini

Crisp baguette, melty brie, and a spoonful of jewel-red cranberry — the colors of Christmas on one little toast. Chopped walnuts and rosemary make them look bakery-pretty with almost no effort.

Ingredients

  • 1 baguette, sliced 1/2-inch thick
  • 8 oz brie cheese, sliced
  • 3/4 cup cranberry sauce
  • 1/3 cup walnuts, chopped and toasted
  • 1 tablespoon fresh rosemary, chopped, plus small sprigs
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C), brush the baguette slices with olive oil, and toast 6 to 8 minutes until crisp.
  2. Top each toast with a slice of brie and bake 4 to 5 minutes more, until just melty.
  3. Spoon a little cranberry sauce over each crostini.
  4. Scatter with the walnuts and rosemary and serve warm.

5. Buffalo Cauliflower Bites

All the fiery, buttery flavor of buffalo wings in a poppable veggie bite. Bake them until crisp, sauce them well, and put the ranch right alongside for cooling dips.

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower, cut into bite-size florets (about 5 cups)
  • 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 3/4 cup hot sauce
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted
  • Ranch dressing, for serving
  • Salt, to taste

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Whisk the flour, water, garlic powder, and a pinch of salt into a batter, then toss the florets through it until coated.
  3. Arrange in one layer and bake 20 minutes, flipping halfway, until lightly crisp.
  4. Stir the hot sauce and melted butter together, toss the florets through it, and bake 10 minutes more until the edges crisp up.
  5. Serve hot with ranch for dipping.

6. Jalapeño Popper Dip

Everything you love about jalapeño poppers — creamy, cheesy, and a little fiery — in one bubbling, scoopable dip. A buttery crumb topping gives every bite a crunch.

Ingredients

  • 16 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 4 to 5 jalapeños, seeded and diced
  • 1/2 cup cooked bacon bits
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted
  • Tortilla chips or crackers, for serving

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Mix the cream cheese, sour cream, cheddar, jalapeños, and half the bacon until smooth, then spread into a baking dish.
  3. Stir the breadcrumbs with the melted butter and scatter over the top with the remaining bacon.
  4. Bake 20 to 25 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  5. Rest 5 minutes and serve warm with chips or crackers.

7. Mini Meatball Sliders

Saucy little meatballs tucked into soft mini buns under a blanket of mozzarella. Put the whole tray in the middle of the table and let everyone grab their own.

Ingredients

  • 24 small meatballs, homemade or frozen
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 24 mini slider buns
  • Fresh basil leaves

Directions

  1. Simmer the meatballs in the marinara for 10 to 12 minutes, until hot all the way through.
  2. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and set the bottom halves of the buns in a baking dish.
  3. Place a meatball and a spoonful of sauce on each bun and cover generously with mozzarella.
  4. Add the bun tops and bake 8 to 10 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbling.
  5. Tuck a basil leaf into each slider and serve warm.

8. Coconut Shrimp with Sweet Chili Sauce

Crisp, golden shrimp in a crunchy coconut crust, made for dunking in sweet chili sauce. Sweet, savory, and gone in minutes — make a double batch for a crowd.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined, tails on
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup shredded coconut
  • 3/4 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Sweet chili sauce, for serving

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and set a rack over a lined baking sheet.
  2. Stir the coconut and panko together. Season the flour with salt and pepper.
  3. Dredge each shrimp in flour, dip in egg, then press firmly into the coconut mixture.
  4. Arrange on the rack, spritz with oil, and bake 10 to 12 minutes, flipping once, until crisp and golden (or fry 2 to 3 minutes).
  5. Serve hot with sweet chili sauce for dipping.

9. Prosciutto-Wrapped Asparagus

Tender asparagus spears wrapped in salty prosciutto and roasted until the edges crisp. A shave of Parmesan at the end keeps them simple, elegant, and completely irresistible.

Ingredients

  • 24 asparagus spears, woody ends trimmed
  • 12 slices prosciutto, halved lengthwise
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/4 cup shaved Parmesan cheese
  • Black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Wrap each asparagus spear in a spiral of prosciutto and place on the sheet.
  3. Drizzle with the olive oil and grind over a little black pepper.
  4. Roast 10 to 12 minutes, until the asparagus is crisp-tender and the prosciutto edges crisp.
  5. Shower with Parmesan and serve warm or at room temperature.

10. Caramelized Onion and Gruyère Tartlets

Sweet, slow-cooked onions and nutty Gruyère in flaky puff pastry — little tartlets that taste like French onion soup in party dress. The onions can be caramelized a day ahead.

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
  • 3 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 cup shredded Gruyère cheese
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Cook the onions in the butter over low heat for 25 to 30 minutes, stirring often, until deep golden and sweet.
  2. Stir in the thyme and season with salt and pepper; let cool slightly.
  3. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Cut the pastry into 12 squares and press into a mini muffin pan (or set on a lined sheet).
  4. Fill each pastry with onions and top with Gruyère.
  5. Bake 15 to 18 minutes until puffed and golden; serve warm.

11. Baked Crab Rangoon

Crisp wonton wrappers around a creamy, garlicky crab filling — all the takeout-favorite flavor, baked instead of fried. Serve them straight from the tray while they are still crackling.

Ingredients

  • 24 wonton wrappers
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 6 oz crab meat, drained and picked over
  • 3 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • Oil spray

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Mix the cream cheese, crab, spring onions, garlic powder, and soy sauce until smooth.
  3. Place a teaspoon of filling in the center of each wrapper, moisten the edges, and fold and pinch to seal.
  4. Arrange on the sheet, spritz with oil, and bake 10 to 12 minutes until crisp and golden.
  5. Rest 2 minutes and serve warm.

12. Mini Beef Empanadas

Golden half-moons with a savory beef, onion, and olive filling — hearty, hand-held, and perfect for making ahead. A quick warm-through in the oven brings back the crisp pastry.

Ingredients

  • 12 empanada discs, or pie dough cut into rounds
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1/2 cup green olives, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 large egg, beaten, for egg wash
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Brown the beef with the onion, drain, and stir in the olives, paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper; let cool.
  2. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  3. Spoon a tablespoon of filling onto each disc, fold into a half-moon, and crimp the edges with a fork.
  4. Brush with the beaten egg and bake 18 to 20 minutes until deep golden.
  5. Rest a few minutes and serve warm.

13. Falafel Bites with Tahini

Herby, golden falafel bites with crisp edges and soft centers, drizzled with lemony tahini. They are the Christmas party appetizers even the meat-lovers keep reaching for — fresh, warm, and completely moreish.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and patted dry
  • 1 cup fresh herbs (parsley and cilantro), chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 3 tablespoons flour
  • 1/3 cup tahini
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Salt, to taste

Directions

  1. Pulse the chickpeas, herbs, garlic, cumin, flour, and salt in a food processor until coarse — never a purée — then chill 20 minutes.
  2. Roll into walnut-size balls and flatten slightly.
  3. Bake on an oiled sheet at 425°F (220°C) for 20 to 25 minutes, flipping once, until golden (or fry 3 to 4 minutes).
  4. Whisk the tahini, lemon juice, a pinch of salt, and enough cold water to make a pourable sauce.
  5. Drizzle over the warm falafel and serve.

14. Greek Salad Skewers

Cool cucumber, juicy tomatoes, salty feta, and olives on a pick — a fresh, no-cook bite to balance all the rich dishes. A little oregano and good olive oil are all the dressing they need.

Ingredients

  • 1 cucumber, cut into chunks
  • 24 cherry tomatoes
  • 24 cubes feta cheese
  • 24 Kalamata olives
  • 1/2 red onion, cut into small wedges
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Short skewers or cocktail picks

Directions

  1. Thread a cucumber chunk, a cherry tomato, a feta cube, an olive, and a piece of red onion onto each pick.
  2. Arrange the skewers on a serving platter in neat rows.
  3. Drizzle with the olive oil and sprinkle with the oregano and a pinch of flaky salt.
  4. Chill until serving.

15. Pesto Palmiers

Crisp, swirly pastry spirals with basil pesto and Parmesan in every layer. They bake from a simple roll-and-slice log and look far fancier than they are.

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
  • 1/3 cup basil pesto
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Flour, for dusting

Directions

  1. Roll the pastry into a rectangle on a lightly floured surface and spread with the pesto, then scatter over the Parmesan.
  2. Roll both long sides in to meet at the center, wrap, and chill 20 minutes until firm.
  3. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  4. Slice the log into 1/2-inch pieces, lay them cut-side up on a lined sheet, and bake 12 to 15 minutes until crisp and golden.
  5. Cool on a rack and serve.

16. Cheese Straws

Flaky, cheesy twists with a gentle cayenne warmth — dangerously snackable with a drink in hand. Make plenty, because they disappear by the handful.

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 cup finely shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
  • 1 large egg, beaten

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Brush the pastry with the beaten egg and scatter over the cheddar, cayenne, and sesame seeds, pressing lightly.
  3. Cut into 1/2-inch strips and twist each strip several times.
  4. Lay the twists on the sheet and bake 12 to 15 minutes until crisp and deep golden.
  5. Cool completely before serving.

17. Chicken Caesar Wonton Cups

Crisp wonton cups filled with creamy chicken Caesar salad and a snowfall of Parmesan. Crunchy, cool, and easy to eat in two bites — fill them at the very last minute.

Ingredients

  • 24 wonton wrappers
  • 2 cups cooked chicken, chopped
  • 2 cups romaine lettuce, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup Caesar dressing
  • 1/3 cup shaved Parmesan cheese
  • Oil spray
  • Black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C), press the wrappers into a mini muffin pan, spritz with oil, and bake 6 to 8 minutes until crisp and golden; cool in the pan.
  2. Toss the chicken, romaine, and Caesar dressing together with a grind of black pepper.
  3. Fill each cup with the salad just before serving.
  4. Top with the shaved Parmesan and serve at once.

18. Marinated Olives and Feta

Warm olive oil with orange peel, rosemary, and chili poured over olives and feta — fragrant, glossy, and made for scooping up with bread. The longer it sits, the better it gets.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed olives
  • 1 cup feta cheese, cut into cubes
  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • Peel of 1 orange, in strips
  • 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes
  • Crusty bread, for serving

Directions

  1. Warm the olive oil over low heat with the orange peel, rosemary, and chili for 3 to 4 minutes, until fragrant — do not let it fry.
  2. Place the olives and feta in a shallow bowl or dish.
  3. Pour the warm, scented oil over the top and toss gently.
  4. Marinate at least 30 minutes (or overnight in the fridge; bring back to room temperature before serving).
  5. Serve with crusty bread for scooping up the oil.

19. Sweet Potato Rounds with Goat Cheese and Cranberry

Roasted sweet potato rounds with creamy goat cheese, tart cranberries, and thyme — colorful little stacks that brighten the whole platter. A tiny dot of honey on each one makes them shine, and they have earned their place among the most-requested Christmas party appetizers on this table.

Ingredients

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, cut into 1/2-inch rounds
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 oz goat cheese, softened
  • 1/3 cup dried cranberries
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 2 teaspoons honey
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Toss the sweet potato rounds with the olive oil, salt, and pepper, then roast 20 to 25 minutes, flipping once, until tender with browned edges.
  3. Cool 5 minutes, then spread or pipe a little goat cheese onto each round.
  4. Top with the dried cranberries and thyme.
  5. Add a tiny dot of honey to each and serve warm or at room temperature.

20. Teriyaki Chicken Skewers

Glossy, caramelized chicken skewers with ginger and garlic in every bite. Finish with sesame seeds and spring onions and serve them hot off the tray.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1/3 cup soy sauce
  • 3 tablespoons honey
  • 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
  • 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  • Wooden skewers, soaked in water

Directions

  1. Stir the soy sauce, honey, ginger, and garlic together and marinate the chicken for at least 30 minutes (or overnight).
  2. Thread the chicken onto the soaked skewers. Simmer the leftover marinade for 2 minutes to make a glaze.
  3. Grill or broil over medium-high heat for 10 to 12 minutes, turning and brushing with the glaze, until caramelized and cooked through.
  4. Scatter with the sesame seeds and spring onions and serve hot.

21. Baked Mozzarella Sticks

Crisp crumb, stretchy center — the freezer is the secret to mozzarella sticks that hold together in a hot oven. Serve them the second they come out, with warm marinara for dunking.

Ingredients

  • 12 mozzarella cheese sticks
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 1 1/2 cups seasoned breadcrumbs
  • Marinara sauce, warmed, for serving

Directions

  1. Dredge each mozzarella stick in flour, dip in egg, then roll in breadcrumbs.
  2. Repeat the egg and breadcrumb coating a second time for an extra-crisp shell.
  3. Freeze on a lined tray for at least 1 hour, until completely solid — do not skip this step.
  4. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and bake the frozen sticks 8 to 10 minutes, until crisp and just melting.
  5. Serve at once with the warm marinara.

22. Roasted Red Pepper Hummus Platter

Silky hummus with sweet roasted peppers, swirled wide in a bowl so every scoop catches the good olive oil and paprika. Surround it with crunchy dippers and guests will happily hover.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained
  • 1 cup roasted red peppers
  • 1/3 cup tahini
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, plus more for drizzling
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • Salt, to taste
  • Crudités and pita, for serving

Directions

  1. Blend the chickpeas, roasted peppers, tahini, lemon juice, garlic, and salt, streaming in the olive oil and a splash of ice water until silky smooth.
  2. Taste and adjust the lemon and salt.
  3. Swirl into a wide, shallow bowl and finish with a drizzle of olive oil and the paprika.
  4. Surround with the crudités and pita and serve.

23. Burrata and Prosciutto Crostini

Creamy burrata and silky prosciutto ribbons over fig-sweetened toasts, finished with fresh basil. Rich and fresh at the same time, and ready in the time it takes to toast the bread.

Ingredients

  • 1 baguette, sliced and toasted
  • 2 balls burrata cheese (about 8 oz)
  • 6 slices prosciutto, torn into ribbons
  • 1/4 cup fig jam, or 2 tablespoons honey
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Directions

  1. Brush the baguette slices with olive oil and toast at 400°F (200°C) for 6 to 8 minutes until golden.
  2. Spread each toast with a thin layer of fig jam.
  3. Tear the burrata and spoon it generously over the toasts.
  4. Drape a ribbon of prosciutto over each one.
  5. Finish with basil leaves and a grind of black pepper; serve at once.

24. Bacon Jam Crostini

Sweet-savory bacon jam — onions melted down with bacon and brown sugar — spooned warm onto crisp toasts. A little cream cheese underneath makes each bite extra smooth.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb bacon, chopped
  • 2 onions, finely diced
  • 1/3 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup strong coffee, or 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1 baguette, sliced and toasted
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened (optional)

Directions

  1. Cook the bacon until crisp; lift it out and pour off all but a spoonful of the fat.
  2. Cook the onions in the bacon fat over low heat for about 20 minutes, until soft and jammy.
  3. Return the bacon with the brown sugar and coffee (or vinegar) and simmer low for 10 to 15 minutes, until thick and glossy.
  4. Spread the toasts with cream cheese, if using, and spoon the warm jam over the top.
  5. Serve warm or at room temperature.

25. Herb and Cheese Stuffed Peppadew Peppers

Sweet, tangy little peppers piped full of herbed cheese with lemon zest and chives — bright, poppable, and no cooking at all. Chill them well so the filling stays neat and firm.

Ingredients

  • 24 jarred Peppadew sweet piquanté peppers, drained and patted dry
  • 8 oz herbed goat cheese or Boursin-style cheese, softened
  • 2 tablespoons chives, finely chopped, plus more to garnish
  • 1 teaspoon lemon zest
  • Black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Stir the cheese, chives, lemon zest, and black pepper together until smooth.
  2. Spoon into a piping bag (or a small freezer bag with the corner snipped) and pipe into each pepper.
  3. Garnish with the extra chives and a little more lemon zest.
  4. Chill at least 30 minutes and serve cold.

Party Hosting Tips

  • Plan on 10 to 12 appetizer pieces per guest when appetizers are the meal, and a few less when a main course follows.
  • Keep hot bites warm in a low oven, around 200°F (95°C), and bring them out in small batches so they stay crisp.
  • Put out one big make-ahead dip so you are not cooking at the door when guests arrive — the whipped feta or the hummus on this list is perfect for this.
  • Label dishes for guests with allergies, noting nuts, dairy, shellfish, and gluten wherever they appear.

Final Thoughts

With these Christmas party appetizers on the table — something crisp, something creamy, something fresh, and plenty that can be made ahead — hosting feels easy again. Pick five or six favorites for a smaller gathering, or make the full spread when the whole crew is coming over.

Save this list for your holiday party, pour something festive, and do not be surprised when guests ask for the recipes before they have even taken their coats off.
