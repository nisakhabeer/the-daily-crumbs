25+ Christmas Thumbprint Cookies with Sweet Fillings

Oct 24, 2026

Thumbprint cookies are the little jewels of the cookie tin — a buttery ball, a thumbprint well, and a shining spoonful of something sweet. For a tin that never gets boring, Christmas thumbprint cookies keep the base simple and change what goes in the middle, from classic raspberry jam to salted caramel and spiced eggnog glaze.

Start with the base dough in Item 1 and treat it like a master recipe: make one batch, roll it into balls, and work through the fillings in any order you like. This collection of Christmas thumbprint cookies runs from jammy classics and glossy ganache to nut-crusted, spiced and tropical twists, so there is a well worth filling for careful bakers, small helpers and anyone building a cookie box to share.

1. Classic Raspberry Thumbprints

This is the master recipe for the whole list: a tender, buttery base with a glossy well of raspberry jam in the middle. Master this one batch and every variation below is just a new dough twist or a different spoonful in the center.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 2/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg yolk, at room temperature
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 1/2 cup seedless raspberry jam

Directions

  1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale and fluffy, then beat in the egg yolk and vanilla. Stir in the flour and salt until a soft dough forms.
  2. Roll the dough into 1-inch balls, set them on a parchment-lined baking sheet and chill for 10 minutes so the edges stay neat.
  3. Press a well into the center of each ball with your thumb or the back of a round measuring spoon, then spoon raspberry jam into each well.
  4. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–14 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden and the jam looks glossy and set. Cool on the pan for 5 minutes, then move to a rack.

2. Apricot Thumbprints

Apricot jam bakes into sunny, golden centers with a gentle tang that loves the plain buttery base. A single slivered almond pressed on top makes them look like they came from a bakery box.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1)
  • 1/2 cup apricot jam or preserves
  • 24 slivered almonds (optional)

Directions

  1. Make the base dough, roll it into 1-inch balls and chill on a parchment-lined sheet for 10 minutes.
  2. Press a well into each ball with the back of a round measuring spoon and fill each well with apricot jam.
  3. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–14 minutes, until the edges are pale gold and the jam shines. Press a slivered almond onto each center while warm, if you like, and cool on a rack.

3. Chocolate Ganache Thumbprints

These bake as plain buttery shells, then get filled with warm, pourable ganache that sets into a truffle-like center. They are the richest bite in the tin and the first to vanish.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1)
  • 4 oz semisweet chocolate, finely chopped
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream
  • Pinch of fine salt

Directions

  1. Make the base dough, roll into 1-inch balls, chill 10 minutes, then press a deep well into each ball with your thumb.
  2. Bake empty at 350°F (175°C) for 11–13 minutes, until set and just golden at the edges. Re-press the wells gently while warm, then cool completely.
  3. Pour the hot cream over the chopped chocolate, rest 2 minutes, then stir smooth with the salt. Spoon the warm ganache into the cooled wells and let it set for about 1 hour before serving.

4. Salted Caramel Thumbprints

A pool of amber caramel in a buttery well, finished with a pinch of flaky salt, tastes like a candy-shop truffle in cookie form. Fill them after baking so the caramel stays soft and glossy.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1)
  • 1/2 cup thick caramel sauce
  • Flaky sea salt, for sprinkling

Directions

  1. Make the base dough, roll into 1-inch balls, chill 10 minutes, then press a well into each ball with the back of a round measuring spoon.
  2. Bake empty at 350°F (175°C) for 11–13 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden. Re-press the wells while warm and cool completely.
  3. Spoon caramel sauce into each well, let it settle for 15 minutes, then finish with a tiny pinch of flaky sea salt on every cookie.

5. Lemon Curd Thumbprints

Bright, tangy lemon curd turns the plain base into a little sunshine bite that cuts through a rich cookie spread. A snowfall of powdered sugar right before serving makes them party-ready.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1)
  • 1/2 cup lemon curd
  • Powdered sugar, for dusting

Directions

  1. Make the base dough, roll into 1-inch balls, chill 10 minutes, then indent each ball with your thumb or a spoon back.
  2. Bake empty at 350°F (175°C) for 11–13 minutes, until pale gold at the edges. Re-press the wells while warm and cool completely.
  3. Fill each well with lemon curd, then dust the tops lightly with powdered sugar just before serving.

6. Strawberry Jam Thumbprints

Strawberry jam gives a sweeter, pinker center than the classic raspberry, and kids tend to claim these first. Stir the jam well before spooning so every well fills evenly.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1)
  • 1/2 cup strawberry jam

Directions

  1. Make the base dough, roll it into 1-inch balls and chill on a parchment-lined sheet for 10 minutes.
  2. Press a well into each ball with your thumb and spoon strawberry jam into the wells, filling them just to the rim.
  3. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–14 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden and the jam is set and shiny. Cool on the pan 5 minutes, then on a rack.

7. Nutella Thumbprints

A glossy spoonful of chocolate-hazelnut spread in a buttery well needs nothing more than a shower of chopped hazelnuts. Fill these after baking so the spread stays silky instead of drying out.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1)
  • 1/2 cup chocolate-hazelnut spread (Nutella)
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped toasted hazelnuts

Directions

  1. Make the base dough, roll into 1-inch balls, chill 10 minutes, then press a well into each ball with the back of a round measuring spoon.
  2. Bake empty at 350°F (175°C) for 11–13 minutes, until set and lightly golden. Re-press the wells while warm and cool completely.
  3. Spoon chocolate-hazelnut spread into each well, swirling the top, and sprinkle with chopped hazelnuts while the filling is still soft.

8. Peanut Butter and Jelly Thumbprints

The lunchbox classic, reborn as a thumbprint: a soft peanut butter dough with a jewel of jelly in the middle. Grape is traditional, strawberry is sweeter — either way, they disappear fast.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar, plus extra for rolling
  • 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 1/2 cup grape or strawberry jelly

Directions

  1. Beat the butter, peanut butter and both sugars until fluffy, then beat in the egg and vanilla. Stir in the flour, baking soda and salt to make a soft dough.
  2. Roll into 1-inch balls, roll lightly in extra sugar if you like, and chill on a parchment-lined sheet for 10 minutes. Press a well into each ball with your thumb — or press a fork crosshatch around the rim for the classic look.
  3. Spoon jelly into the wells and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 11–13 minutes, until the edges are set and the jelly is glossy. Cool on the pan for 5 minutes, then on a rack.

9. Cherry Thumbprints

A drop of almond extract in the dough makes cherry preserves taste even more like cherries. The deep red centers look like ornaments against the pale, buttery rounds.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1), with 1/2 tsp almond extract added with the vanilla
  • 1/2 cup cherry preserves

Directions

  1. Make the base dough with the almond extract, roll it into 1-inch balls and chill for 10 minutes on a parchment-lined sheet.
  2. Press a well into each ball with your thumb or a spoon back and fill the wells with cherry preserves.
  3. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–14 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden and the preserves bubble gently. Cool on a rack.

10. White Chocolate Thumbprints

Sweet, creamy white chocolate pools in the wells like little puddles of snow. A pinch of red sprinkles scattered over the top while the filling is soft makes them instantly festive.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1)
  • 4 oz white chocolate, chopped (or 2/3 cup white chocolate chips)
  • 2 tbsp heavy cream
  • Red sprinkles, for decorating

Directions

  1. Make the base dough, roll into 1-inch balls, chill 10 minutes, then press a well into each ball with the back of a round measuring spoon.
  2. Bake empty at 350°F (175°C) for 11–13 minutes, until the edges are just golden. Re-press the wells while warm and cool completely.
  3. Melt the white chocolate with the cream until smooth, spoon into the wells, and scatter red sprinkles over the top while the filling is still soft. Let set for about 30 minutes.

11. Peppermint Kiss Thumbprints

Sugar-rolled buttery rounds get crowned with a striped peppermint kiss the moment they leave the oven. The candy softens into the well and sets with a pretty swirl on top.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1)
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar, for rolling
  • 24 peppermint kiss candies, unwrapped

Directions

  1. Make the base dough and roll it into 1-inch balls, then roll each ball in granulated sugar and chill for 10 minutes on a parchment-lined sheet.
  2. Press a shallow well into each ball and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10–12 minutes, until the edges are just set.
  3. Immediately press a peppermint kiss into the center of each warm cookie, pushing down until it sits snugly in the well. Cool completely so the candy firms back up.

12. Eggnog Thumbprints

Nutmeg in the dough and a creamy eggnog glaze in the well turn these into the cookie version of a holiday toast. Finish with the lightest dusting of extra nutmeg.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1), with 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg stirred into the flour
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 2–3 tbsp eggnog
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg, plus extra for dusting

Directions

  1. Make the nutmeg base dough, roll into 1-inch balls, chill 10 minutes, then press a well into each ball with your thumb.
  2. Bake empty at 350°F (175°C) for 11–13 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden. Re-press the wells while warm and cool completely.
  3. Whisk the powdered sugar, eggnog and nutmeg into a thick glaze, spoon it into the wells, and dust with a little extra nutmeg. Let the glaze set for about 30 minutes.

13. Cranberry Orange Thumbprints

Orange zest perfumes the dough and tart cranberry fills the center for the brightest, most jewel-toned cookie on the tray. They taste like the holidays in two bites.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1), with 1 tsp finely grated orange zest beaten in with the butter
  • 1/2 cup whole-berry cranberry sauce or cranberry jam

Directions

  1. Make the orange-zest base dough, roll it into 1-inch balls and chill for 10 minutes on a parchment-lined sheet.
  2. Press a well into each ball with the back of a round measuring spoon and fill with cranberry sauce or jam.
  3. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–14 minutes, until the edges are golden and the filling is glossy and set. Cool on a rack.

14. Almond Thumbprints

Ground almonds in the dough plus a whisper of almond extract make these taste like a bake-shop classic, and raspberry jam is the traditional partner. The crumb is tender with the faintest nutty chew.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 2/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 1 tsp almond extract
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup finely ground almonds or almond flour
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 1/2 cup raspberry jam

Directions

  1. Beat the butter and sugar until fluffy, then beat in the egg yolk and almond extract. Stir in the flour, ground almonds and salt to form a soft dough.
  2. Roll into 1-inch balls, chill for 10 minutes on a parchment-lined sheet, then press a well into each ball with your thumb.
  3. Fill the wells with raspberry jam and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–14 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden and the jam is set. Cool on a rack.

15. Pecan Thumbprints

These wear a crunchy coat of chopped pecans, so every bite starts crisp and ends buttery. Fill the wells with jam for a classic finish, or with caramel for a praline-style twist.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1)
  • 1 egg white, lightly beaten
  • 1 cup finely chopped pecans
  • 1/2 cup raspberry jam or caramel sauce

Directions

  1. Make the base dough and roll it into 1-inch balls. Dip each ball in beaten egg white, then roll in chopped pecans until well coated, and set on a parchment-lined sheet.
  2. Chill for 10 minutes, then press a well into each ball with the back of a round measuring spoon, pressing the nut crust gently back into place.
  3. If using jam, fill the wells now and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 13–15 minutes, until the nuts are toasted and the jam is set. If using caramel, bake the shells empty, cool, then fill.

16. Vanilla Shortbread Thumbprints

No egg in this dough, just butter, sugar, flour and plenty of vanilla, so the crumb is sandy, tender and almost melting. Any jam works here — pick your favorite jar.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 1/2 cup jam of choice

Directions

  1. Beat the butter and powdered sugar until smooth and creamy, then mix in the vanilla. Stir in the flour and salt until the dough just comes together.
  2. Roll into 1-inch balls, chill for 10 minutes on a parchment-lined sheet, then press a well into each ball with your thumb or a spoon back.
  3. Fill the wells with jam and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–14 minutes, until the edges are set but still pale. Cool on the pan for 10 minutes — shortbread is fragile while warm — then move to a rack.

17. Double Chocolate Thumbprints

A dark cocoa dough filled with creamy white chocolate gives the prettiest contrast on the whole list. They look dramatic on a platter and taste like a candy bar in cookie form.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1), made with 1 3/4 cups flour plus 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 4 oz white chocolate, chopped
  • 2 tbsp heavy cream

Directions

  1. Make the base dough with the cocoa powder swapped in, roll into 1-inch balls, chill 10 minutes, then press a well into each ball with the back of a round measuring spoon.
  2. Bake empty at 350°F (175°C) for 11–13 minutes, until the tops look dry and set. Re-press the wells while warm and cool completely.
  3. Melt the white chocolate with the cream until smooth and spoon it into the cooled wells. Let the filling set for about 30 minutes before stacking or serving.

18. Chai Spice Thumbprints with Apple Butter

Cardamom, cinnamon and ginger warm up the base dough, and a spoonful of apple butter tastes like the inside of a pie. These smell incredible while they bake.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1), with spices stirred into the flour: 1 tsp ground cinnamon, 1/2 tsp ground cardamom, 1/2 tsp ground ginger and a pinch of black pepper
  • 1/2 cup apple butter

Directions

  1. Make the spiced base dough, roll it into 1-inch balls and chill for 10 minutes on a parchment-lined sheet.
  2. Press a well into each ball with your thumb and fill the wells with apple butter, keeping the filling just below the rim.
  3. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–14 minutes, until the edges are firm and the apple butter looks set and slightly darkened. Cool on a rack.

19. Blackberry Thumbprints

Blackberry jam gives a deep purple center with a wild, slightly tart flavor, and a little lemon zest in the dough makes the berries sing. Strain out the seeds if you like a smoother fill.

Ingredients

20. Fig and Walnut Thumbprints

Fig jam brings a honeyed, almost caramel depth, and a walnut half pressed on top makes each cookie look like a little present. These are the grown-ups’ favorite in the tin.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1)
  • 1/2 cup fig jam
  • 24 walnut halves

Directions

  1. Make the base dough, roll it into 1-inch balls and chill for 10 minutes on a parchment-lined sheet.
  2. Press a well into each ball with your thumb and fill the wells with fig jam.
  3. Press a walnut half gently onto the jam in each well, then bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–14 minutes, until the edges are golden and the jam is set. Cool on a rack.

22. Orange Marmalade Thumbprints

Bittersweet orange marmalade gives these a grown-up shine, with little flecks of peel glowing in the wells. Extra zest in the dough doubles down on the citrus.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1), with 1 tsp finely grated orange zest beaten in with the butter
  • 1/2 cup orange marmalade

Directions

  1. Make the orange-zest base dough, roll it into 1-inch balls and chill for 10 minutes on a parchment-lined sheet.
  2. Press a well into each ball with your thumb and fill the wells with marmalade, stirring the jar first so the peel distributes evenly.
  3. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–14 minutes, until the edges are golden and the marmalade shines. Cool on a rack.

23. Cream Cheese Thumbprints

Cream cheese in the dough makes these extra tender with the faintest tang, like cheesecake in cookie form. Use any jam you love — raspberry keeps it classic.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 1/2 cup jam of choice

Directions

  1. Beat the butter and cream cheese until smooth, then beat in the sugar and vanilla. Stir in the flour and salt to make a soft dough.
  2. Roll into 1-inch balls and chill for 15 minutes on a parchment-lined sheet — this dough is softer than the base, so do not skip the chill. Press a well into each ball with the back of a round measuring spoon.
  3. Fill the wells with jam and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–14 minutes, until the edges are just set and barely golden. Cool on the pan for 5 minutes, then on a rack.

24. Coconut Lime Thumbprints

A tropical detour for the cookie tin: chewy coconut in the dough and a tart spoonful of lime curd in the middle. They taste like a mini key lime pie with a coconut crust.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1), with 1/2 cup sweetened shredded coconut folded in and the flour reduced by 2 tbsp
  • 1/2 cup lime curd
  • Extra toasted coconut, for sprinkling (optional)

Directions

  1. Make the coconut base dough, roll into 1-inch balls, chill 10 minutes, then press a well into each ball with your thumb.
  2. Bake empty at 350°F (175°C) for 12–14 minutes, until the coconut edges are lightly toasted. Re-press the wells while warm and cool completely.
  3. Fill each well with lime curd and finish with a pinch of toasted coconut, if you like. Serve within a day for the brightest flavor.

25. Hot Chocolate Thumbprints

All the comfort of a mug of cocoa in one cookie: a cocoa dough, a cloud of marshmallow fluff and a final drizzle of chocolate. The grand finale of the list, and worth every step.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch buttery thumbprint base dough (Item 1), made with 1 3/4 cups flour plus 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 3/4 cup marshmallow fluff
  • 24 mini marshmallows
  • 2 oz semisweet chocolate, melted, for drizzling

Directions

  1. Make the cocoa base dough, roll into 1-inch balls, chill 10 minutes, then press a deep well into each ball with the back of a round measuring spoon.
  2. Bake empty at 350°F (175°C) for 11–13 minutes, until the tops look dry and set. Re-press the wells while warm and cool completely.
  3. Spoon marshmallow fluff into each well and top with a mini marshmallow. Either leave the tops soft and pillowy, or set the pan under a hot broiler for 15–30 seconds, watching closely, until the marshmallows turn golden.
  4. Drizzle with melted chocolate and let everything set for about 20 minutes before serving.

Thumbprint Tricks

The best Christmas thumbprint cookies come from cool dough and the right fill at the right moment. Keep these four steady tricks in mind for every batch on the list.

  • Chill the rolled balls 10 minutes before indenting — warm dough cracks at the edges
  • Use the back of a round measuring spoon for perfect, even wells
  • Re-press the wells gently halfway through baking if they puff up
  • Fill jam thumbprints BEFORE baking so the jam sets, and creamy fillings AFTER so they stay silky

Final Thoughts

Christmas thumbprint cookies earn their sparkle because one simple dough can wear so many centers — jam today, caramel tomorrow, a snowy cap of white chocolate next week. Build a tin with five or six favorites, tuck a few of each into gift boxes, and keep a small stash by the kettle for quiet December afternoons.

Whichever you choose, these Christmas thumbprint cookies freeze well, pack well and disappear fast, so make the double batch. Happy filling, and happy Christmas baking.
