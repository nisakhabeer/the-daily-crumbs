25+ Easy Christmas Finger Foods for a Crowd

Oct 21, 2026

Hosting a holiday party is so much easier when the food practically serves itself. These Christmas finger foods are made for mingling — guests can grab a bite with one hand and keep the conversation flowing, no sit-down dinner required.

This collection of 25+ easy Christmas finger foods for a crowd has something for everyone: make-ahead dips, warm baked bites, and no-cook platters that look as good as they taste. Most are ready in 30 minutes or less, so you can spend less time in the kitchen and more time enjoying the party.

1. Cranberry Brie Bites

Sweet-tart cranberry sauce and creamy melted brie tucked into a crisp little cup — these Christmas finger foods disappear from the platter in minutes. A tiny sprig of rosemary makes each one look party-ready with zero extra effort.

Ingredients

  • 1 package (30 count) mini phyllo cups
  • 8 oz brie cheese, rind removed, cut into small cubes
  • 1 cup whole-berry cranberry sauce
  • 1/3 cup chopped walnuts, toasted
  • Fresh rosemary sprigs, for garnish

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and arrange the phyllo cups on a rimmed baking sheet.
  2. Drop a cube of brie into each cup and spoon a little cranberry sauce over the top.
  3. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, until the brie is melted and bubbly.
  4. Shower with the chopped walnuts and a tiny sprig of rosemary, then serve warm.

2. Pigs in a Blanket

The little bites everyone reaches for first. Buttery crescent dough wrapped around savory cocktail sausages bakes up golden and crisp — simple, nostalgic, and always gone before halftime.

Ingredients

  • 1 package (about 24) cocktail sausages
  • 1 can (8 oz) refrigerated crescent roll dough
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Unroll the crescent dough, pinch the seams together, and cut it into thin strips.
  3. Wrap one strip around each sausage and place seam-side down on the sheet.
  4. Brush with the beaten egg, sprinkle with sesame seeds, and bake 12 to 15 minutes until deep golden.

3. Christmas Tortilla Pinwheels

Creamy, colorful spirals that you can roll up the night before and slice just before guests arrive. The roasted red peppers and spinach give them a naturally festive look.

Ingredients

  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup roasted red peppers, chopped and patted dry
  • 2 cups baby spinach leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Stir the garlic powder, salt, and pepper into the softened cream cheese.
  2. Spread the mixture evenly over each tortilla, edge to edge.
  3. Layer on the spinach and scatter the chopped red peppers over the top.
  4. Roll each tortilla up tightly, wrap in plastic, and chill at least 1 hour.
  5. Trim the ends and slice into 1-inch pinwheels to serve.

4. Caprese Skewers

Juicy tomatoes, creamy mozzarella, and fresh basil on a pick — all the flavor of a caprese salad in a single bite. A drizzle of balsamic glaze makes them shine on the platter.

Ingredients

  • 24 cherry tomatoes
  • 24 small mozzarella balls (bocconcini), drained
  • 24 fresh basil leaves
  • 2 tablespoons balsamic glaze
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Thread one cherry tomato, one folded basil leaf, and one mozzarella ball onto each small skewer or pick.
  2. Arrange the skewers on a serving platter.
  3. Drizzle with the olive oil and balsamic glaze, then season lightly with salt and pepper.
  4. Serve chilled or at room temperature.

5. Classic Deviled Eggs

The retro party staple that never goes out of style. Tangy, creamy filling piped into tender egg whites, finished with a dusting of paprika for that classic look.

Ingredients

  • 12 large eggs
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika, plus more for dusting
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped chives
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Cover the eggs with cold water, bring to a boil, then cook 10 minutes; cool in ice water and peel.
  2. Halve the eggs lengthwise and scoop the yolks into a bowl.
  3. Mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard, salt, and pepper until smooth.
  4. Pipe or spoon the filling back into the whites.
  5. Dust with paprika and scatter the chives over the top before serving.

6. Slow-Cooker Cranberry Meatballs

A handful of ingredients, one slow cooker, zero fuss. These glossy, sweet-tangy meatballs stay warm for hours, which makes them a dream Christmas finger food for a big holiday party.

Ingredients

  • 1 bag (32 oz) frozen meatballs
  • 1 can (14 oz) whole-berry cranberry sauce
  • 1 bottle (12 oz) chili sauce
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons orange juice

Directions

  1. Whisk the cranberry sauce, chili sauce, brown sugar, and orange juice in the slow cooker until smooth.
  2. Add the frozen meatballs and stir to coat them well.
  3. Cover and cook on low for 3 to 4 hours, stirring once halfway through.
  4. Serve straight from the slow cooker set to warm, with cocktail picks alongside.

7. Stuffed Mushrooms

Tender mushroom caps filled with a garlicky, cheesy mixture and baked until golden. They are warm, savory, and just the right size for popping in one bite.

Ingredients

  • 24 medium mushrooms, stems removed and chopped
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/3 cup breadcrumbs
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and arrange the mushroom caps on a baking sheet.
  2. Mix the cream cheese, Parmesan, chopped stems, garlic, and parsley.
  3. Spoon the filling into each cap and sprinkle breadcrumbs over the tops.
  4. Bake 18 to 20 minutes, until golden and bubbling, and serve warm.

8. Cheesy Pull-Apart Christmas Tree Bread

Soft, buttery dough balls stuffed with mozzarella and arranged in a Christmas tree shape — pull one off and the cheese stretches beautifully. Serve it with warm marinara for dipping.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pizza dough, at room temperature
  • 8 oz mozzarella cheese, cut into 3/4-inch cubes
  • 4 tablespoons melted butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 cup marinara sauce, warmed, for dipping

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a large baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Pinch off pieces of dough, flatten each one, place a mozzarella cube in the center, and seal into balls.
  3. Arrange the balls in a tree shape on the sheet, with the sides touching.
  4. Bake 15 to 18 minutes until puffed and golden.
  5. Stir the garlic and parsley into the melted butter, brush it over the warm bread, and serve with the marinara.

9. Spinach Dip Wonton Cups

All the creamy, cheesy goodness of spinach dip, baked into crisp little wonton cups. They hold their shape on a buffet table and are sturdy enough for one-handed snacking.

Ingredients

  • 24 wonton wrappers
  • 10 oz frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and press the wonton wrappers into a mini muffin pan.
  2. Bake 5 to 7 minutes until lightly golden; set aside.
  3. Stir the spinach, cream cheese, sour cream, Parmesan, salt, and pepper until smooth.
  4. Fill each cup with the spinach mixture and bake 8 more minutes until warmed through.

10. Baked Brie with Cranberry and Pecans

A whole wheel of brie wrapped in flaky puff pastry and baked until it oozes at the first cut — a true showstopper among Christmas finger foods. Tart cranberry and buttery pecans are the perfect holiday topping.

Ingredients

  • 1 wheel (8 oz) brie cheese
  • 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
  • 1/2 cup cranberry sauce
  • 1/3 cup chopped pecans, toasted
  • 1 large egg, beaten

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Place the brie in the center of the pastry, spoon the cranberry sauce over the top, and sprinkle with the pecans.
  3. Fold the pastry up around the brie, sealing the seams, and place seam-side down.
  4. Brush with the beaten egg and bake 20 to 25 minutes until deep golden.
  5. Rest 10 minutes before serving with crackers or crostini.

11. Antipasto Skewers

Salty salami, creamy cheese, briny olives, and sweet tomatoes threaded in a pretty repeating pattern. They are no-cook, endlessly customizable, and look gorgeous lined up on a platter.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz salami slices, folded
  • 8 oz cheese cubes (mozzarella or provolone)
  • 1 cup green olives, pitted
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes
  • Fresh basil leaves

Directions

  1. Thread the ingredients onto small skewers in a repeating pattern: salami, cheese, olive, tomato, basil.
  2. Arrange the skewers on a platter in neat rows.
  3. Cover and chill until party time, up to 4 hours ahead.

12. Loaded Potato Skins

Crispy potato shells piled with melted cheddar and bacon, finished with cool sour cream and spring onions. Hearty, cheesy, and built for feeding a crowd.

Ingredients

  • 6 medium russet potatoes
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup cooked bacon bits
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, plus salt for baking

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C); rub the potatoes with oil and salt and bake 50 minutes until tender.
  2. Halve lengthwise and scoop out most of the flesh, leaving a 1/4-inch shell.
  3. Fill each shell with cheddar and bacon, then bake 10 minutes until melted and crisp.
  4. Dollop with sour cream and scatter the spring onions over the top.

13. Honey Garlic Chicken Wings

Crispy baked wings tossed in a sticky, sweet-savory honey garlic glaze. They are messy in the best way — set out plenty of napkins and watch them vanish.

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs chicken wings, split and patted dry
  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tablespoons butter

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and arrange the wings on a rack over a lined baking sheet.
  2. Bake 40 to 45 minutes, flipping once, until crisp and cooked through.
  3. Simmer the honey, soy sauce, garlic, and butter in a saucepan until slightly thickened.
  4. Toss the hot wings in the glaze and serve immediately.

14. Mini Quiches

Flaky little shells filled with a rich egg custard, ham, and spinach. They taste fancy but take minutes to assemble, and they are just as good at room temperature.

Ingredients

  • 24 mini pastry shells (or 1 pie crust, cut and fitted into a mini muffin pan)
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup diced ham
  • 1/2 cup chopped spinach

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and arrange the shells on a baking sheet.
  2. Whisk the eggs and cream, then stir in the cheddar, ham, and spinach.
  3. Pour the mixture into the shells, filling each about three-quarters full.
  4. Bake 15 to 18 minutes, until just set and lightly golden; cool 5 minutes before serving.

15. Shrimp Cocktail Cups

A party classic, shrunk down to single servings. Plump shrimp hang on the rim of little cups filled with zesty cocktail sauce — elegant, easy, and no double-dipping worries.

Ingredients

  • 24 large cooked shrimp, peeled with tails on
  • 3/4 cup ketchup
  • 3 tablespoons prepared horseradish
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley
  • Lemon wedges, for serving

Directions

  1. Stir the ketchup, horseradish, and lemon juice to make the cocktail sauce.
  2. Spoon a tablespoon of sauce into each small cup or shot glass.
  3. Hang one shrimp on the rim of each cup.
  4. Sprinkle with parsley and serve with the lemon wedges.

16. Tomato Bruschetta Toasts

Crunchy garlic-rubbed toasts piled high with juicy marinated tomatoes and basil. Fresh, bright, and a lovely contrast to all the rich holiday bites on the table.

Ingredients

  • 1 baguette, sliced 1/2-inch thick
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes, diced
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh basil
  • 1 clove garlic, halved
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, plus more for brushing
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic glaze

Directions

  1. Brush the baguette slices with olive oil and toast at 400°F (200°C) for 6 to 8 minutes until golden.
  2. Rub the warm toasts with the cut side of the garlic.
  3. Toss the tomatoes with the basil, olive oil, and a pinch of salt; spoon onto the toasts.
  4. Finish with a drizzle of balsamic glaze and serve right away.

17. Sausage Rolls

Buttery, shattering puff pastry wrapped around savory sausage meat — the party favorite that deserves a spot at every holiday table. Best served warm from the oven.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb sausage meat
  • 2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Roll each pastry sheet into a rectangle and lay a log of sausage meat down the center.
  3. Brush one edge with egg, roll up tightly, and seal.
  4. Cut into 2-inch pieces, brush with egg, and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
  5. Bake 20 to 25 minutes until puffed, crisp, and golden.

18. Smoked Salmon Crostini

Silky smoked salmon over lemony cream cheese on crisp toast, finished with dill and capers. They look elegant, take minutes to build, and need no cooking at all.

Ingredients

  • 1 baguette, sliced and toasted
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 8 oz smoked salmon
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh dill
  • 1 teaspoon lemon zest
  • 2 tablespoons capers, drained

Directions

  1. Spread a layer of cream cheese over each toasted slice.
  2. Drape a ribbon of smoked salmon on top.
  3. Sprinkle with the lemon zest, dill, and capers.
  4. Arrange on a platter and serve chilled.

19. Christmas Cheese Ball

A creamy, garlicky cheese ball rolled in pecans and parsley for a festive green-and-gold finish. Make it a day ahead — it slices and spreads even better after a night in the fridge.

Ingredients

  • 16 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded sharp cheddar
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 3/4 cup chopped pecans, toasted
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
  • Crackers, for serving

Directions

  1. Beat the cream cheese, cheddar, and garlic powder until smooth.
  2. Shape into a ball, wrap tightly, and chill at least 2 hours.
  3. Roll the chilled ball in the pecans and parsley to coat.
  4. Set on a platter with crackers and serve.

20. Veggie Christmas Tree Platter

Broccoli florets arranged in a tree shape and dotted with cherry tomato “baubles” — a fresh, colorful centerpiece that kids love. The ranch dip bowl sits at the base like a tree trunk.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes
  • 2 cups cauliflower florets
  • 2 carrots, cut into sticks
  • 1 bell pepper, cut into sticks
  • 1 cup ranch dip

Directions

  1. Spoon the ranch dip into a small bowl and place it at the bottom center of a large platter.
  2. Build a triangle of broccoli above the bowl to form the tree.
  3. Dot cherry tomatoes over the broccoli like ornaments.
  4. Fan the cauliflower, carrot, and pepper sticks around the base as extra dippers.

21. Garlic Parmesan Pretzel Bites

Soft, chewy bites with that classic pretzel chew, tossed in garlicky butter and a snowfall of Parmesan. Irresistible warm, and the coarse salt gives them the real-deal flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pizza dough
  • 4 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon coarse salt
  • 8 cups water + 1/4 cup baking soda, for boiling (optional)

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and cut the dough into 1-inch pieces.
  2. Boil the pieces in the baking-soda water for 30 seconds each (optional but recommended), then drain.
  3. Bake 12 to 15 minutes until deep golden brown.
  4. Toss the warm bites in the melted butter and garlic, then shower with Parmesan and salt.

22. Mini Taco Cups

Crunchy wonton shells loaded with seasoned beef and melty cheddar, finished with sour cream and salsa. All the fun of taco night in two perfect bites.

Ingredients

  • 24 wonton wrappers
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/2 cup salsa

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C); press the wonton wrappers into a muffin pan and bake 5 minutes until crisp.
  2. Brown the beef, drain, and stir in the taco seasoning with a splash of water.
  3. Fill each cup with beef and a pinch of cheddar; bake 5 more minutes until the cheese melts.
  4. Top with sour cream and salsa just before serving.

23. Chicken Satay Skewers

Tender strips of chicken marinated in coconut milk and curry, grilled until lightly charred and served with a creamy peanut sauce. Sweet, savory, and a little bit exotic on a holiday table.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs chicken breast, cut into strips
  • 1/2 cup coconut milk
  • 2 teaspoons curry powder
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon honey

Directions

  1. Marinate the chicken in the coconut milk, curry powder, and soy sauce for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Thread the strips onto soaked wooden skewers.
  3. Grill or bake at 425°F (220°C) for 10 to 12 minutes, turning once, until cooked through.
  4. Warm the peanut butter with the lime juice, honey, and a splash of water to make the sauce.
  5. Serve the skewers with the peanut sauce for dipping.

24. Chocolate-Dipped Pretzel Rods

Salty pretzel rods dipped in chocolate and rolled in Christmas sprinkles — the sweet-and-salty no-bake treat that doubles as an edible gift. Kids love helping with the decorating.

Ingredients

  • 24 pretzel rods
  • 12 oz milk or white chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup Christmas sprinkles

Directions

  1. Melt the chocolate in a microwave-safe bowl in 30-second bursts, stirring until smooth.
  2. Dip each pretzel rod two-thirds of the way into the chocolate, letting the excess drip off.
  3. Immediately roll in or sprinkle with the Christmas sprinkles.
  4. Stand upright or lay on parchment until the chocolate sets, about 20 minutes.

25. Christmas Brownie Bites

Fudgy two-bite brownies crowned with swirls of frosting and a flurry of sprinkles. Baked in a mini muffin pan, they come out perfectly portioned for a party platter.

Ingredients

  • 1 box (18 oz) brownie mix, plus eggs and oil per package
  • 1 cup frosting (vanilla or chocolate)
  • 1/4 cup Christmas sprinkles
  • 2 tablespoons powdered sugar, for dusting

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a mini muffin pan.
  2. Prepare the brownie batter per the package and fill each cup two-thirds full.
  3. Bake 12 to 14 minutes until just set; cool completely in the pan.
  4. Pipe or spread frosting on each bite, add the sprinkles, and dust lightly with powdered sugar.

Make-Ahead Tips for Serving a Crowd

  • Make dips, pinwheels, and the cheese ball a day ahead — most Christmas finger foods taste even better after the flavors meld overnight.
  • Keep warm bites hot in a low oven (200°F) or a slow cooker set to warm until guests arrive.
  • Set out cold platters, skewers, and crostini last so everything stays fresh and crisp.
  • For a finger-food-only party, plan on 10 to 12 pieces per guest, and offer a mix of hearty, fresh, and sweet bites.
